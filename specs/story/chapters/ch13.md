# Chapter 13: "Second Chances"

## Summary

A highland fortress controlled by Zael, a Wyvern Rider partially corrupted by the System. Zael is aggressive, scared, and hearing voices — his CRP is at 40 and ticking upward. The party must seize the fortress gate while keeping Zael alive long enough to recruit him. If Ren reaches Zael at 5 HP or less and talks, Zael defects. If Zael is killed, he drops a rare weapon. If ignored entirely, he escapes and becomes the Ch14 boss instead. This is the first chapter where CRP management becomes a metagame — Purify staves are mentioned as a future tool, and the party must decide whether saving a corrupted enemy is worth the tactical risk. Mid-chapter introduces the concept that corruption can be fought, but only at cost.

## Mid-Battle Events

### Turn 2 — Zael's Aggression

Zael dives from the sky and attacks the nearest unit. His AI is Aggressive — he comes to the player, fast and hard. Axe + lance, Wyvern Rider mobility. But his attacks are erratic — sometimes he hesitates mid-swing.

- **Zael**: "GET AWAY FROM MY FORTRESS! The buzzing — the BUZZING in my skull — you're making it WORSE!"
- **Ren**: "He thinks we're causing the corruption."
- **Kira**: "The corruption tells you that. Everyone else is the enemy. Everything outside your head is the threat. I remember."
- **Senna**: "His CRP is climbing. 43 now. If we can weaken him without killing him, Ren might be able to talk him down."

**Mechanical note**: Zael is Aggressive AI but switches to Survival (retreats at 30% HP). The player must carefully reduce him to 5 HP or less without killing him. Over-damage is a real risk — his erratic stat shifts from CRP mean damage forecasts are slightly unreliable.

### Turn 5 — The Choice Crystallizes

Zael drops below 15 HP and his AI shifts to Survival — he retreats to the fortress roof, cornered. His wyvern huddles beside him.

- **Zael**: *(panting, holding his head)* "I fly for whoever feeds my wyvern. What are you offering?"
- **Rook**: "He's asking to be recruited."
- **Kira**: "Or he's asking for mercy. Same thing, from where he's standing."
- **Senna**: "His CRP is at 48. Still below the point of no return. If Ren can reach him—"
- **Orin**: "A REDEMPTION ARC! In the THIRD act! The dramatic timing is EXQUISITE!"
- **Ren**: "I need to get up there. Clear a path."

**Mechanical note**: Ren must reach Zael's tile and use Talk when Zael is at 5 HP or less. Zael's Survival AI means he won't attack if no one is adjacent, but his CRP ticks up each turn. If CRP hits 60 before recruitment, the window closes and he becomes unrecruitable.

### Turn 7 — Recruitment or Death

**If Ren reaches Zael at ≤5 HP:**

- **Ren**: "You don't have to fight for whoever feeds your wyvern. You can fight for yourself."
- **Zael**: "Myself? There's barely anything LEFT of myself. The buzzing—"
- **Ren**: "I know about the buzzing. I know about the corruption. We have people who can help."
- **Zael**: *(long pause)* "...If the wyvern eats, I fight."
- **Ren**: "Deal."

**[Zael joins. Wyvern Rider, Level 19. Axe/lance. CRP 48 (ticking). Flying.]**

**If Zael is killed:** He drops a Killer Lance. No recruitment. "The buzzing... stopped." He dissolves.

**If Zael escapes:** He flees the map. Becomes the Ch14 boss instead of The Hollow.

### Turn 9 — Fortress Push

With or without Zael, the party pushes toward the fortress gate. Corrupted soldiers hold the courtyard. The gate is the Seize objective.

- **Voss**: "Standard fortress defense. Two chokepoints. Archers on the walls."
- **Bram**: "I'll take the left chokepoint. Give me a healer and I'll hold."
- **Faye**: "I'll keep him standing. Lira, take the right side."
- **Lira**: "On it."
- **Senna**: "The corrupted soldiers here are lower-tier than Ch11. The corruption is spreading thin — quantity over quality."
- **Ren**: "Good. We use that. Push hard, seize fast."

## Boss Fight — Fortress Warden

If Zael was recruited, the fortress Warden — a heavily armored General — holds the gate as the final obstacle. If Zael was NOT recruited, Zael himself is the boss (see Turn 7 alternate path).

**Warden (if Zael recruited):** General, Tier 4 (Unaware). Level 19, HP 55. Lance/axe. Boss AI, on gate tile with terrain bonus. Two armored escorts.

- **Pre-combat**:
  - **Warden**: "The commander's gone mad and surrendered. Fine. I'll hold this gate myself."
  - **Ren**: "You're fighting for a corrupted fortress."
  - **Warden**: "I'm fighting because it's my post. Corruption, politics, whatever you call it — I hold the gate."

**Mechanical note**: The Warden is a straightforward General fight — high DEF, low RES. Magic is effective. His escorts can be baited away. Standard seize-the-gate tactics apply.

- **Post-combat**:
  - **Warden**: *(on one knee)* "Gate's yours. I held it as long as I could."
  - **Voss**: "You held it well."
  - **Warden**: "...Thanks. That's more than my actual commanders ever said."

**[Ren seizes the fortress gate. Chapter complete.]**

## Story Beats Delivered

- Zael's conditional recruitment: the first "recruit or kill" choice with lasting consequences across chapters
- CRP management introduced as active metagame — players must balance dealing damage with not over-killing
- Zael's character: aggressive exterior hiding fear, "buzzing in my skull" motif, wyvern bond as emotional anchor
- Kira's parallel to Zael drawn explicitly — she spread corruption, he resists it, both are partially corrupted
- Purify staves teased as the solution to CRP, threading into Ch14's monastery
- If Zael is killed or ignored, Ch14 boss changes — first major branching consequence
- Lighter moments return briefly (Bram's optimism, Orin's theatrics) amid the dark arc tone
- The "second chance" theme: can corrupted people be saved? Zael is the first test case
- Seize objective returns, grounding the chapter in standard SRPG mechanics despite the CRP complexity
