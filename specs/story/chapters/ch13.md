# Chapter 13: Kiri Gorge

*Not yet implemented.*

## Summary

A gorge crossing under a wyvern ambush, and the party's first recruit who is already
carrying the blight.

## Intent

- **Objective**: seize the far bridgehead
- **New mechanic**: verticality — fliers holding ledges, bows as the answer
- **Joins**: a wyvern rider at Corruption 40. A conditional recruit and a standing
  risk: if their Corruption reaches 100 they turn mid-battle, permanently. The player
  is told this plainly the moment they join — no hidden trap.
- **Boss**: a Kurogane sniper captain holding the high ledge, left behind by her own
  army's retreat, who has decided the gorge is hers now.
