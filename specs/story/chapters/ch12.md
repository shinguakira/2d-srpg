# Chapter 12: The Silent Village

***Built and playable.***

## Summary

**Shimba**, up the Sarz — a village the blight took whole and left standing.
Nothing burnt, nothing broken, the bread still on the tables. Everyone in it is a
revenant.

## Intent

- **Objective**: survive / escape, at night, under fog of war
- **The horror is domestic**, not monstrous. The party fights through people in
  aprons, in a village laid out exactly like the ones they have spent eleven
  chapters defending.
- **Mirelle's chapter.** Her rule from Ch7 — *do not look at their faces* — stops being
  possible here, and she is the one who has to keep saying the rites anyway.
- **No boss.** The absence of a commander is the point: for the first time there is
  nothing on the map to negotiate with, threaten, or spare.
