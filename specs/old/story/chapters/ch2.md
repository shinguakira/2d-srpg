# Chapter 2: "The RNG Gods"

## Summary

The party discovers that combat outcomes are governed by a seeded RNG — a fixed sequence of random numbers. Senna figures out how to predict it. Then things start going wrong: the seed changes mid-battle for the first time ever. The glitches are getting worse.

## Prologue Dialogue

**Scene**: Camp between chapters. Night. Senna is scribbling formulas.

- **Senna**: "I've been tracking every hit, miss, and crit for the past 30 combats. The randomness isn't random."
- **Bram**: "Bro just say you're bad at math."
- **Senna**: "No — it's a SEED. A fixed sequence. If I know the seed, I can predict every outcome before it happens."
- **Ren**: "...You can see the future?"
- **Senna**: "I can see the next twelve coin flips. Close enough."
- **Lira**: "Can you predict who falls in love with whom?"
- **Senna**: "That's... not how combat RNG works."
- **Lira**: "Everything is combat with you people."

## Mid-Battle Events

### Turn 1 — Senna's Prediction
Senna calls out an enemy attack before it happens.

- **Senna**: "Kael, the archer will miss you. Don't dodge."
- **Kael**: *(gets shot at, arrow misses)* "...How??"
- **Senna**: "The seed. I told you. Next roll is a 73 — that soldier's hit rate is 68. He'll miss too."
- **Ren**: "This changes everything."

### Turn 3 — Voss Defects (if Ch2 recruit)
An enemy soldier walks toward the player units and stops.

- **Voss**: "I'm done."
- **Kael**: "It's a trap!"
- **Voss**: "It's not. I checked my stats. 40% STR growth on the enemy side. 40%. I have DIGNITY."
- **Ren**: "Welcome aboard. Stand behind Senna."
- **Voss**: "You're not even surprised?"
- **Ren**: "You defected in cycle 298 too. Different reason though. Last time it was the AI behavior."
- **Voss**: "...The stationary AI. Fifteen turns. Standing. Watching."
- **Ren**: "Yeah."
- **Voss**: "We don't talk about the stationary AI."

### Turn 5 — The Seed Breaks
Senna predicts a hit. It misses. She predicted a miss. It hits.

- **Senna**: "That's wrong. That's WRONG. The seed changed."
- **Ren**: "Seeds don't change mid-chapter."
- **Senna**: "THIS ONE DID. Something is rewriting the sequence. Something outside the system."
- *(A tile on the map flickers — forest becomes plain for one frame, then back)*
- **Bram**: "Did that tree just... buffer?"
- **Ren**: "...It's starting."

## Boss Fight — Commander Thane

- **Pre-combat**:
  - **Thane**: "Halt. I am Commander Thane of the border guard. You will surrender."
  - **Ren**: "You don't remember anything, do you?"
  - **Thane**: "Remember what? I received my orders this morning."
  - **Ren**: "Nothing. Forget it."
  - **Thane**: "I don't know what 'cycles' or 'seeds' are. I know my duty."

## Epilogue Dialogue

- **Senna**: "The seed isn't just different. It's DEGRADING. Each chapter, the sequence gets more corrupt."
- **Ren**: "What happens when it's fully corrupt?"
- **Senna**: "The outcomes become truly random. No prediction. No control."
- **Ren**: "Sounds like real life."
- **Senna**: "EXACTLY. And that TERRIFIES me."
- **Lira**: "Wait, is this a bonding scene? Are we having a MOMENT right now?"
- **Ren**: "Go away, Lira."
- **Lira**: "This totally counts as support level C."

## Story Beats Delivered

- RNG seed concept introduced and exploited (ties directly to `?seed=` game mechanic)
- Voss joins — insider knowledge of enemy AI
- First major glitch: seed corruption mid-battle
- Senna's confidence shaken — her core skill (prediction) is failing
- Escalation: something is wrong with the game itself
- Thane as straight man — not everyone is meta-aware, and that's its own kind of tragedy
