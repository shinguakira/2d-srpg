# Chapter 15 Map: "The Rally"

## Overview
- Grid size: 20×22
- Tileset: Fortress wall, stone floor, mountain, road, plain, forest, fort, throne, void (corrupted patches)
- Terrain focus: Largest map so far — highland fortress with three entry points, inner courtyard, corrupted terrain patches

## Layout
A massive highland fortress occupying most of the map. The south (rows 16-21) is the staging area: open plains with road tiles and scattered forest. Three entry points lead into the fortress. The main gate (row 14, cols 10-11) faces south — heaviest resistance but shortest path. The east sally port (row 10, col 20) is a narrow 1-tile entrance guarded by archers. The west supply tunnel (row 12, col 1) enters through forest — lightly guarded but has trap tiles (damage on step). The outer fortress (rows 8-15) has walls, corridors, and two fort defensive positions at (12, 6) and (12, 16). The inner courtyard (rows 4-7, cols 6-15) is a large open area with three corrupted terrain patches: (5, 8), (5, 12), and (6, 10) — stable corruption that Shigeru can overwrite with EMB. Ghast's throne sits at (1, 11) in the north keep behind a final wall with a gate at (3, 10-11). Three Escort AI guards hold the courtyard at (4, 8), (4, 14), and (5, 11).

## Tactical Notes
- **Three-pronged assault**: The map rewards splitting into three teams. Main gate (strongest force — Shigeru, Gareth, Halvar), east sally port (ranged team — Bryn, Corwin, a healer), west tunnel (utility team — Fenn for traps, Kira, a healer). Elin and Zael can fly between groups as needed.
- **Coordinated escort guards**: Ghast's 3 escort Knights in the courtyard reposition when one is hit. The counter-strategy: use Viviane's Dance to give a strong attacker two actions, burst one guard down before the others react, then push through the gap.
- **Ghast's DEF wall**: Ghast has extreme DEF on a throne tile. Physical attacks bounce. Magic (Lisette, Kira, Elara) is the primary damage source. Elara's light magic deals bonus damage against the corrupted terrain nearby. Flanking Ghast to deny throne bonuses is the advanced play.
- **EMB expenditure**: Turn 8's event lets Shigeru overwrite 3 corrupted patches — this is narrative-critical but has no direct tactical effect beyond clearing terrain hazards. The cost is paid in story (lost memories), not in combat stats.
- **Promoted enemy debut**: The Turn 10 reinforcements include a Paladin and Sage — the first promoted enemies. They hit harder and have more HP than anything before. Save strong units and Dance for this wave.
- **Trap tiles**: The west tunnel has damage traps at (12, 2-3). Fenn can disarm them. Without Fenn, units take 10 damage per step — painful but survivable. The tunnel is lightly guarded as compensation.
- **Rout objective**: Every enemy must fall. No shortcuts — the party must clear all three zones plus reinforcements. Efficient splitting and Viviane's Dance are essential to avoid running out of steam.
