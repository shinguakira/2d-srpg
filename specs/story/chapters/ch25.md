# Final: Takeshi, Emperor of Ash

*Not yet implemented.* Full profile in [bosses.md](../bosses.md).

## Summary

The argument, and then the fight.

## Intent

- **Objective**: defeat Takeshi
- **Boss**: Takeshi, carrying the Blackflame in his own body. Multi-phase — the man
  first, then progressively less of the man.

## Writing rules for the finale

- **Takeshi never concedes the argument.** He is not talked down by being proved
  wrong, because he is not wrong about the thing he is right about: the seal was a
  delay, and it has already failed once.
- **Shigeru does not win the debate. He refuses it.** His position is that a delay
  bought four centuries, that four centuries is four centuries, and that Takeshi's
  alternative requires one man to stay certain forever.
- **The best outcome is a choice, not a defeat** (`takeshiSpared`). The player earns
  the chance to make Takeshi put it down by having spared people all campaign — the
  ending flags are the receipts for that, not arbitrary collectibles.
- **The cost is fixed and known since Ch19.** Relighting the seal spends all 347
  embers. The Flamebrand goes dark on the shrine floor and Shigeru leaves it there.
  Amagi's kings do not carry it again, and that is the correct ending for it.
