# Chapter 14: The Shrine on the Cape

***Built and playable.***

## Summary

South to **Suza**, the oldest shrine-town on the island, where the southern Sacred
Flame still burns and the keepers have no intention of letting an army near it.

## Intent

- **Objective**: defend the shrine precinct
- **Terrain**: cape ground — loose sand on the beaches below, steep cliff paths
  above. Cavalry is punished, so the chapter forces the player to fight with the
  units they have been benching.
- **The first ally who is not a stray.** Everyone recruited so far had nowhere else
  to go. The Suza **wardens** have a shrine, a wall and four centuries of not
  caring who sits on the throne at Ilza — Shigeru has to argue for a kingdom that
  no longer exists.
- **Joins**: **a Suza warden** — light magic, which is what the blight's own units
  are weak to, and the hands that can read a ward off a stone in Ch24.

**Warden, not keeper.** Ch19 protects the *Shirato keeper* and that is a
different person with an ending flag attached (`shiratoKeeperSaved`). Two roles,
two words, kept apart on purpose.
- **Boss**: a Kurogane column that reached the cape first and made the same argument
  with better manners and more gold.
