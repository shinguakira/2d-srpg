# Chapter 11: The Ash Road

*Arc 3 opener. Not yet implemented.*

## Summary

North, onto the track the Colossus's ash ran down. The party leaves country that
still has people in it. Grey fields, a village where the wells are cold, and the
first use of Blighted terrain as a map-wide hazard rather than a set piece.

## Intent

- **Objective**: escape/advance — cross the ash road with everyone alive
- **New mechanic at scale**: Corruption accrues from terrain across most of the map.
  Standing still is punished; the payoff is knowing the one rule Kanna has proven —
  running water stops it.
- **Joins**: a shaman who has been living on the ash road and has learned to answer
  the blight in its own language. Distrusted by Hina on doctrinal grounds and by
  Kanna on evidential ones, which is a healthier argument than either expects.
- **Boss**: a Kurogane column commander retreating *south*, who cannot understand
  why anyone would be walking the other way.

## Beats

The first village where the revenants wear clothes the party recognises — towns they
passed through in Arc 1. Kanna's new book gets its second entry.
