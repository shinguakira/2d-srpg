# Chapter 2: "The Defector"

## Summary

Border conflict at a garrison outpost. Commander Thane holds the crossing — a professional cavalier who doesn't know what a "cycle" is and doesn't care. The chapter's real event is Voss: an enemy soldier who walks toward the player mid-battle and defects, sick of standing in place for 347 cycles. Meanwhile, Senna begins studying combat outcomes and realizes the randomness isn't random — it's seeded. She doesn't crack it yet, but the suspicion is planted. First chapter with villages to visit and the Seize objective in a fortified context.

## Mid-Battle Events

### Turn 1-2 — The Garrison Approach

Players advance across open terrain toward the garrison. Enemy soldiers hold defensive positions. Two villages sit on the flanks — side objectives.

- **Kael**: "The bridge is narrow — only one unit can cross at a time."
- **Senna**: "Or we flank through the shallow water. Slower, but we can approach from two sides."
- **Ren**: "Kael, you have the MOV to reach the eastern village. Check on the civilians."
- **Kael**: "How do you know there are civilians in danger?"
- **Ren**: "...Smoke."
- **Senna**: *(writing in her notebook)* "Interesting. That archer had a 72% chance to hit Bram. He missed. The soldier had 65%. He hit. I'm tracking these."

### Turn 3 — Voss Defects

An enemy soldier on the garrison wall walks toward the player units. He crosses the entire map slowly — stationary AI overridden. He stops in front of Ren.

- **Voss**: "I'm done."
- **Kael**: "It's a trap!"
- **Voss**: "It's not. I've been standing on that wall tile for... I don't know how long. Days? Years? I can't tell anymore. But I know I'm tired of standing still."
- **Ren**: "Welcome aboard, Voss."
- **Voss**: "You're not even surprised."
- **Ren**: "You defected in cycle 298 too. Different reason that time — you were angry about the AI."
- **Voss**: "...The stationary AI. Fifteen turns. Standing. Watching. While my commander fights and I just STAND THERE."
- **Ren**: "Yeah."
- **Voss**: "We don't talk about the stationary AI."
- **Bram**: "Wait — the ENEMIES know they're standing still? They know that's WEIRD?"
- **Voss**: "Most of them don't. I just... noticed. At some point. I don't know when."

**[Voss joins the party. Soldier class. Lance equipped.]**

### Turn 4 — Senna's Observation

Senna's been tracking hit rates in her notebook.

- **Senna**: "Seventeen combats tracked. The outcomes aren't random."
- **Ren**: "Senna—"
- **Senna**: "There's a pattern. A sequence. Every hit and miss follows a fixed order. Like the combat is using a... a seed. A pre-determined list of numbers."
- **Ren**: "...You figured that out in seventeen combats?"
- **Senna**: "I figured out the HYPOTHESIS. Proving it will take more data. But if I'm right — if the outcomes are seeded — I can eventually predict every attack before it happens."
- **Lira**: "Can you predict romantic chemistry?"
- **Senna**: "I can predict that you'll ask that. Because you ask it every chapter."
- **Lira**: "...We've only had two chapters."
- **Senna**: "Exactly."

## Boss Fight — Commander Thane

Commander Thane. Cavalier (lance), Guard AI (radius 4), Tier 4 (Unaware). Level 7, 38 HP. Mounted. Guards the area around the garrison throne. Drops Broken Seed on defeat.

- **Pre-combat**:
  - **Thane**: "Halt. I am Commander Thane of the border garrison. State your purpose."
  - **Ren**: "You won't believe me if I tell you."
  - **Thane**: "I don't know what 'save files' or 'cycles' are. I don't care. I received my orders this morning. I will carry them out."
  - **Ren**: "You received those same orders 347 mornings ago."
  - **Thane**: "...Is that a threat?"
  - **Ren**: "No. It's just sad."

**Mechanical note**: Thane is mounted — he has high MOV within his guard radius. He'll chase players who enter his range but return to the throne area if they retreat. His lance beats swords — use Bram (axe) or Senna (magic) for weapon advantage. Players must Seize the throne after defeating him.

- **Post-combat**:
  - **Thane**: *(defeated)* "My garrison... falls..."
  - **Voss**: "He never knew. He did his job every cycle and never once questioned it."
  - **Ren**: "That's most people here, Voss."
  - **Voss**: "I know. That's what made standing still so unbearable — I was the only one who noticed."

**[Ren seizes the garrison throne. Chapter complete.]**

## Story Beats Delivered

- Voss joins — first enemy defection, establishes that enemies have awareness too (some of them)
- Voss's meta-angle: hates the stationary AI behavior, knows something is wrong but can't articulate it
- Senna begins studying the RNG seed — hypothesis formed, not yet proven (seeds don't break until Ch7)
- Broken Seed item dropped by Thane — a physical clue that foreshadows the System's seeded architecture
- Commander Thane as Tier 4 (Unaware) — professional soldier, no meta-awareness, tragic in a different way than Garrek
- Seize objective reinforced — must take the throne after the boss falls
- Villages as side objectives — teach the exploration/reward loop
- Lira's genre-displaced comedy continues (every conversation is potential romance)
- Ren's foreknowledge more obvious — party members are starting to ask questions
