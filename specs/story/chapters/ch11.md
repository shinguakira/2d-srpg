# Chapter 11: The Ash Road

*Arc 3 opener. Not yet implemented.*

## Summary

West and inland, onto the track the Colossus's ash ran down — up the Sasu from
Komoda, into the valley Corwin described in Ch6. The party leaves country that still
has people in it. Grey fields, a village where the wells are cold, and the first use
of Blighted terrain as a map-wide hazard rather than a set piece.

## Intent

- **Objective**: escape/advance — cross the ash road with everyone alive
- **New mechanic at scale**: Corruption accrues from terrain across most of the map.
  Standing still is punished; the payoff is knowing the one rule Lisette has proven —
  running water stops it.
- **Joins**: a shaman who has been living in the grey up the Sasu and has learned to answer
  the blight in its own language. Distrusted by Mirelle on doctrinal grounds and by
  Lisette on evidential ones, which is a healthier argument than either expects.
- **Boss**: a Kurogane column commander retreating *south*, who cannot understand
  why anyone would be walking the other way.

## Beats

The first village where the revenants wear clothes the party recognises — towns they
passed through in Arc 1. Lisette's new book gets its second entry.
