# Characters

20 playable characters across 25 chapters. See [roster.md](roster.md) for join/leave timeline, deployment limits, and per-arc snapshots.

## Naming Convention

Short, punchy names. 3-5 letters. Easy to read on small UI panels and sprite labels.

---

## Original Cast (Ch1-5)

- [Ren](characters/ren.md) — Lord, protagonist, remembers all 347 cycles
- [Kael](characters/kael.md) — Cavalier, the loyal one, dies Ch8
- [Senna](characters/senna.md) — Mage, RNG scientist
- [Bram](characters/bram.md) — Fighter, thinks he's in a fighting game
- [Lira](characters/lira.md) — Cleric, thinks she's in a dating sim
- [Voss](characters/voss.md) — Soldier, enemy defector, joins Ch2

## Arc 1 Recruits (Ch3-5)

- **Nira** — Archer, village hunter. Joins Ch3. Tier 4 (Unaware). Straightforward and earnest. Her lack of awareness makes her a grounding presence.
- **Coda** — Thief, street orphan. Joins Ch4. Tier 3-4 (senses something wrong). Pragmatic survivor. First to notice "something's off" without understanding what.
- **Yuel** — Pegasus Knight, idealist. Joins Ch5. Tier 4 (Unaware). Believes in heroism and justice. Flying unit adds tactical diversity.

## Arc 2 Recruits (Ch6-10)

- **Rook** — Mercenary, sellsword. Joins Ch6. Tier 4 (Unaware). Fights for gold, stays for respect. Classic mercenary arc.
- **Faye** — Troubadour, minor noble. Joins Ch6. Tier 4 (Unaware). Mounted healer. Sheltered background cracking against war's reality.
- **Orin** — Dancer, traveling performer. Joins Ch9. Tier 3 (Genre-Displaced — thinks he's in a rhythm game). The only Dance user. Essential support unit.

## Arc 3 Recruits (Ch11-15)

- **Kira** — Shaman, dark magic defector. Joins Ch11. Tier 2 (Partially Aware). Studied dark magic and saw the System's code. Fled her order. High CRP risk, high potential.
- **Zael** — Wyvern Rider, conditional recruit. Joins Ch13 (if spared). Tier 4 (Unaware). Partially corrupted by the System. Recruitment requires refusing to kill him + dialogue choice. If refused, becomes Ch14 boss.
- **Elara** — Monk, monastery healer. Joins Ch14. Tier 2 (Partially Aware). Light magic user. Spiritual awareness parallels meta-awareness — "I feel the pattern of all things."

## Arc 4 Recruits (Ch16-20)

- **Ghael** — Armor Knight, former boss. Joins Ch18 (if recruited: reduce to ≤5 HP, talk with Ren). Tier 4 (Unaware). Stubborn, proud, but honor-bound. Joins underleveled — requires investment.
- **Echo** — System Construct, unique class. Joins Ch20. Tier 2-3 (aware of system, confused by emotions). Created by the System to understand player behavior. Gained free will. Can equip any weapon. Flat 30% growths.

## Non-Playable Key Characters

- **Garrek** — Ch1 boss, Fighter. Tier 1 (Full Awareness). Has been killed 347 times. The tutorial boss who remembers everything.
- **The System** — Tier 5. The game's runtime. Not a person — a process trying to save everyone by resetting forever.
- **???_CORRUPTED** — Ch24 boss. Kael's save data forced into a combat unit. The System's last attempt to bring Kael back.
