# Chapter 10: The Sands of Kodo

Not implemented.

**Arc 2 close — Takeshi
appears in person.**

## Summary

**Kodo Beach**, one arc after the invasion landed on it: village and farmland
below, a fortified hill above. A protect objective, the first **Blackflame
Colossus**, and then the villain walks down the hill road unarmed and asks for the
sword.

## Setup

- **Map**: 26×18, village and sand below, fortified hill above, water barrier
  with a central bridge, three approach routes
- **Objective**: Defeat Grand Magus Ezrin while protecting Elder Ilse
- **Deploy**: 9 slots, `shigeru` forced
- **Enemies**: 18
- **Boss**: Ezrin (sage) with three escort guards
- **Ally NPC**: Elder Ilse, in the village below, stationary
- **Reinforcements**: 4 on turns 3, 6 and 9
- **Turn 6**: a Blackflame Colossus stands up out of the sand between the village
  and the hill
- **Par**: 20 turns

## Beats

**Prologue.** Elder Ilse keeps the village chronicle: four hundred years, and every
time the Blackflame stirred somebody here wrote it down. Lisette reads the situation
correctly — Ezrin is not garrisoning a hill, he came for the book. Shigeru commits
to both objectives and says why: *"Halvar held a corridor for nine turns so that we
could still afford to do both."*

**Turn 6 — the Colossus.** Grass goes grey in a widening ring and something stands
up out of it: huge, armoured, put together wrong. Lisette makes the distinction that
matters — *"That is not a revenant. A revenant was a person once. This has been
made — assembled, out of several."* It goes straight for the elder, which tells the
party it was sent rather than wandering.

**Colossus death — the trail.** Its ash does not scatter. It runs west along the
ground, against the wind. *"Being recalled. Whatever built that thing wants its
pieces back — and it will lead us straight to the door."* This is the first thing in
the campaign the party can actually follow.

**Boss.** Ezrin read the same signs Lisette reads and kept serving anyway: *"I serve
the empire I was born in, not the truth I found too late to use."*

**Between the boss and the epilogue — the second failed approach.** They are
standing on the only other landing on this coast, and Arn is six kilometres north.
This time there *is* a road: a cliff path north out of the bay, two men wide.

They take it, and it ends. **The Stone Gate** — the headland cut through, filled
with stone and warded, four centuries ago, by their own side. Arn is visible past
it. There is no way round; the sea is on one hand and a hundred metres of rock on
the other.

Keep this short and unexplained. Nobody works out what the gate is for; that is
Ch23's job. What the player takes away is that **the last six kilometres are shut**,
and that the company standing here cannot open them.

**Epilogue — Takeshi.** He comes down the hill road while they are still counting
the wounded. Not in armour. Does not draw. A head taller than Corwin, shaven bald,
both arms burned to the elbow in a pattern like bark. He stops at a polite distance
and waits to be addressed.

**He has just watched them fail to get through.** That is why the scene is here
and not anywhere else: he is offering to carry the sword the six kilometres they
cannot walk it, and both of them know he can, because he came in that way — alone,
by sea, under the arch at low tide. **His offer is not a trap and it is not a
bluff. It is the shortest way, and it is closed to everyone in this company.**

The scene's rules:

- **He is courteous throughout.** He asks Akira not to shield the prince because he
  would rather look at him than at his back.
- **He admits the murder immediately.** He asked King Sadao for the Flamebrand four
  times — *"three more than I have ever asked anyone"* — and killed him for refusing.
- **He corrects the accusation precisely.** He did not let the Blackflame out; he
  took it into himself. It has been quiet for eleven months. The grey ground is the
  cost, and he pays it.
- **He makes the strongest version of his own argument.** The 347 knights bought the
  world four centuries. Four. He intends to end it rather than postpone it again for
  somebody else's grandchildren.
- **His offer is real.** Bring the Flamebrand to Arn — it is the last thing still tied
  to the seal, and the thing inside him keeps reaching for it — and everyone here
  walks away.
- **His threat is a description, not a boast.** It will keep coming, and one morning
  Shigeru will be standing in a grey field working out which of these faces he could
  have kept.

Then he turns and walks back up the road. Nobody raises a bow, and afterwards none
of them can say why.

**Shigeru's refusal is not a rebuttal** — he does not out-argue Takeshi and is not
meant to. He goes west anyway, on his own terms: *"Not because he asked. Because he
is right that it is coming, and I would rather meet it at the shrine than in
somebody's field."*

## Supports

- **Shigeru × Akira** — Akira notices he has not said Halvar's name since the
  fortress. Saying it to the company is not the same as saying it. *"It was the right
  order and it was a terrible thing to have to say, and both of those are going to be
  true for the rest of your life."*
- **Corwin × Bryn** — five engagements in, still claiming it is only for the coin.
  *"The pay is terrible. The hours are worse. The commander apologises to corpses."*
