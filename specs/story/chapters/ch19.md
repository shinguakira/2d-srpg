# Chapter 19: The Second Shrine

*Not yet implemented.*

## Summary

The eastern shrine of **Mitsu**, still burning, and the last keeper alive to tend
it. Both armies arrive at once.

## Intent

- **Objective**: protect the shrine keeper (`shrineKeeperSaved` — an ending flag)
- **Lore payoff**: the keeper explains what the 347 actually did, and what relighting
  a shrine costs. The Flamebrand holds 347 embers; relighting the north spends every
  one of them and the sword goes dark for good.
- **Structural point**: Shigeru learns the price of the best ending *here*, with six
  chapters left to carry it, rather than being handed it as a twist at the end.
