# Chapter 22: Sworn Brothers

*Not yet implemented.*

## Summary

The history chapter. Takeshi and King Sadao were sworn brothers, and Takeshi taught
Sadao to ride. The party fights through the Emperor's oldest regiment — men who
served with both of them and remember the friendship.

## Intent

- **Objective**: boss kill
- **Boss**: the Emperor's oldest general, who stood at the oath-taking, and who tells
  Shigeru exactly what his father and Takeshi were to each other before he dies.
- **Purpose**: make the finale a family matter rather than a set piece. Everything
  the player learns in this chapter should make Ch25 harder to *want* to win.
