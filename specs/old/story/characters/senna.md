# Senna — Mage (The Scientist)

- **Class**: Mage (fire/wind/thunder)
- **Awareness Tier**: 2 (Partial — senses the system layer)

## Personality

Analytical, precise, speaks in formulas and probabilities. Treats the world as a system to be decoded, not a place to live in. Emotionally guarded — numbers are safe, feelings are unpredictable. Finds comfort in patterns and panics when patterns break.

## Meta-Angle

High MAG stat creates a resonance with the system layer. She can "read" parts of the runtime the way a musician hears overtones — not full awareness, but partial perception of the underlying code.

- Decodes the RNG seed in Ch2 and predicts combat outcomes: "Next roll is 73, his hit rate is 68. He'll miss."
- Reads enemy AI behavior flags: "He's set to guard-radius 3, just stay at 4 tiles."
- Speaks about stats and formulas as observable phenomena, not game mechanics — she's DISCOVERED them, not remembered them
- Doesn't know about the cycles or the resets — she sees the code but not the git history

## What She Doesn't Know

- That the world has reset 347 times (Ren's knowledge, not hers)
- That her analytical perception is itself a bug — the System didn't intend for anyone to read the runtime
- That the RNG seed degradation she discovers in Ch2 is a symptom of total system failure, not a local anomaly

## Relationships

- **Ren**: Intellectual sparring partners. Senna discovers what Ren already knows — but through ANALYSIS, not memory. She asks "why does this work this way?" while Ren asks "does it matter?" They need each other: Senna provides understanding, Ren provides context.
- **Kael**: Fond but frustrated. He asks "is 73% good?" and she has to physically restrain herself from a lecture. Secretly admires that he acts on instinct while she's paralyzed by calculation.
- **Bram**: Exasperated. His "frame data" talk is ALMOST correct in ways that drive her insane. "That's not how— actually, the weapon triangle IS kind of a matchup chart. Stop being accidentally right!"
- **Lira**: Dismissive at first ("romance isn't a variable"), but Lira's emotional intelligence consistently predicts character behavior better than Senna's formulas. This bothers Senna deeply.
- **Voss**: Kindred spirits. Both understand the system from the inside — Senna through analysis, Voss through lived experience as an enemy unit. They compare notes. Voss provides the AI behavior data that completes Senna's models.

## Arc

1. **Ch1**: Background presence. Notices patterns but hasn't formalized them yet. "Doesn't it seem like combat outcomes follow a sequence...?"
2. **Ch2**: Peak confidence. Cracks the RNG seed. Predicts combat. Feels like a god. Then the seed changes mid-battle and her world breaks. "That's wrong. That's WRONG."
3. **Ch3**: Operating without certainty for the first time. Her predictions are unreliable. The system data she reads is corrupted. She has to learn to act on incomplete information — which is what everyone else has been doing all along.
4. **Ch4**: Uses her system knowledge not to PREDICT but to HEAL. Discovers the weapon triangle exploit against ???_CORRUPTED. "I don't want to crash the system. I want to fix a bug." Her arc completes: from exploiting the system to repairing it.

## Key Lines

- *"According to my calculations, we have a 73% chance of survival. ...I liked it better when I could round up to 100."*
- *"The seed changed. Seeds don't change. SOMETHING IS REWRITING THE SEQUENCE."* (Ch2 turning point)
- *"I don't want to crash the system. I want to fix a bug."* (Ch4 — discovering the exploit)
