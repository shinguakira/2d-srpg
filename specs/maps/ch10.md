# Chapter 10 Map: "What We Carry"

## Overview
- Grid size: 16×18
- Tileset: Plain, road, village, forest, mountain, fort, wall, throne (hilltop)
- Terrain focus: Village at the base of a fortified hill — dual objective requires splitting forces

## Layout
The map has two distinct zones. The southern half (rows 10-17) is a village: roads, houses (wall tiles), a village square at (14, 9) where Elder Ilse stands as an NPC. Forest patches provide cover along the village edges. The northern half (rows 0-9) is a fortified hill — the slope rises from row 9 (plains) to row 4 (mountain-adjacent fort tiles), with Ezrin's throne-equivalent position at (1, 9) on the hilltop. The hill has three approach routes: a central stairway (cols 8-9, rows 4-9), a winding left path through forest (cols 2-5, rows 3-8), and a right path along a cliff edge (cols 13-15, rows 3-8). Fort tiles at (5, 5) and (5, 13) mark defensive anchors on the side approaches. A corrupted tile spawns at (7, 9) on Turn 6.

## Tactical Notes
- **Dual objective split**: The party must defend Ilse at the village AND push uphill to Ezrin. A minimum of 3 units should stay near the village (including a healer) while the assault team pushes north. Elin's flying mobility lets her flex between zones.
- **Ezrin's Physic self-heal**: Ezrin heals himself below 70% HP. The party must deal burst damage in a single round to prevent recovery. Viviane's Dance on Lisette or Elara enables a double-cast burst strategy — the intended solution.
- **Coordinated guards**: Ezrin's 3 guard soldiers reposition when one is hit. Bait one guard forward with a high-DEF unit (Halvar), then strike the exposed gap with the assault team.
- **System Construct**: The Turn 6 construct is a mid-boss threat with 50 HP, high flat stats, and immunity to status. It ignores terrain and targets Ilse. Wall it off with multiple bodies or burst it down before it reaches the village. It does NOT count toward the main objective.
- **Grief debuff expiry**: The -3 all stats debuff from Ch8 lifts at chapter end. This means the Ezrin fight is the last battle under the debuff — the player's units are at their weakest for the hardest boss yet.
- **Master Seal drop**: Ezrin drops a Master Seal — the first promotion opportunity. This signals the power curve shift into Arc 3 and rewards players who commit to the difficult hilltop assault.
- **Barrier staff**: The chest at (2, 16) contains a Barrier staff (+7 RES). Reaching it requires clearing the right hilltop approach, guarded by a Knight on a fort tile. Worth the effort for the Hollow fight in Ch14.
