# Kael — Cavalier (The Loyal One)

- **Class**: Cavalier (lance, high MOV)
- **Awareness Tier**: 4 (Unaware — reset works perfectly on him)

## Personality

Earnest, brave, treats every battle like his first — because for him, it IS. Has no memory of past cycles. No existential dread. No meta-humor. He's the most "normal" character in a cast of meta-aware weirdos, and that's exactly what makes him special.

He's the heart of the group. The one who says "we can do this" and means it without irony.

## Meta-Angle

He has NONE — and that's the point. In a game full of fourth-wall breaks, Kael is the character who plays it straight. His sincerity creates contrast that makes every meta-joke funnier and every dramatic moment heavier.

- Terrified of forests (movement cost 2) — but he thinks it's just hard terrain, not a game mechanic
- Treats open plains as tactically ideal — "Good ground! Room to charge!"
- Judges cavalry by their horses, not their MOV stat
- When others say "the RNG was kind," he says "we were lucky"

## What He Learns

Mid-game, Ren tells Kael about the loops. Kael's reaction is the most important character moment in the game:

1. **Denial**: "That's impossible. I'd REMEMBER."
2. **Processing**: "So my loyalty, my bravery... I've felt all of it before? Hundreds of times?"
3. **Grief**: "And I lost it. Every time. I lost everything and didn't even know."
4. **Choice**: "I don't care. It's the first time I REMEMBER, so it counts."

He doesn't become meta-aware. He doesn't start making system jokes. He absorbs the truth and chooses to be brave ANYWAY. That choice is more meaningful than any meta-cleverness.

## Relationships

- **Ren**: Childhood friend (in every cycle). Kael senses Ren's exhaustion but attributes it to stress, not 347 lifetimes of trauma. When he learns the truth, he doesn't pity Ren — he's awed that Ren kept going.
- **Bram**: Friendly rivalry. Kael thinks Bram is just enthusiastic. Doesn't understand the "combo meter" talk but respects Bram's fighting spirit.
- **Lira**: Oblivious to her romantic framing. Kael is polite and chivalrous, which Lira reads as "flags triggered." He's just being nice.
- **Senna**: Respects her intelligence, doesn't understand her formulas. "You said 73%? Is that... good?"
- **Voss**: Initially suspicious (former enemy). Grows to respect Voss's choice to defect — Kael understands choosing your own path over following orders.

## Arc

1. **Ch1**: The classic FE cavalier. Charges in, fights bravely, celebrates victory. Pure, uncomplicated.
2. **Ch2**: Notices Ren acting strange. Starts asking questions. "Why do you always know what's coming?"
3. **Ch3**: Learns about the loops. Processes the truth. Chooses courage. Then dies.

## The Death (Ch3, Turn 6-7)

Kael is caught out of position — his high MOV means he's always overextended. A corrupted enemy spawn (the System fighting back) traps him. His greatest strength (mobility) becomes his fatal weakness.

- "Ren... this is the part where I retreat to safety and recover, right?"
- "...Kael."
- "Right? Like in the other games? I just lose this chapter and come back?"
- "This isn't that kind of game."
- "Oh."
- *(beat)*
- "Was I brave this time? In this cycle?"
- "Every time."
- "Then it counted."

He dies believing it mattered. He's right.

## After Death

Kael's absence haunts Ch4. His empty tile. The party avoiding eye contact when someone almost says his name. And then — the Ch4 boss reveals Kael's reconstructed data underneath the corruption. Not a resurrection. A ghost in the machine. The System's failed attempt to bring back the one unit it could never save.

## Key Lines

- *"I don't care if I've done this before. It's the first time I remember, so it counts."*
- *"Good ground! Room to charge!"* (every time he sees open plains)
- *"Was I brave this time? In this cycle?"* (final words)
