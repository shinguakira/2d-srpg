# Chapter 20: The Wood That Was Never Cut

*Arc 4 climax. Not yet implemented.*

## Summary

The old forest on **Taragi** — four centuries of evergreen that no axe has ever
touched, because no layman is permitted to walk under it. The blight's deepest
ground and the densest concentration of revenants in the game.

## Intent

- **Objective**: survive the crossing
- **Terrain**: forest on almost every tile. Movement is halved, sight lines are
  nothing, and the danger zone is unreadable — the chapter is about advancing blind.
- **Escalation**: multiple Colossi, and revenants in armour four centuries out of
  date.
- **The gut punch**: those are the 347. The knights who sealed it are buried under
  this mountain, and they are standing up. The oath was not a clean sacrifice, and
  the shrine songs everyone has been quoting since Ch2 have been lying by omission.
- **Consequence**: this is the chapter that makes Takeshi's position defensible, and
  it lands one arc before the player has to answer it.
