# Chapter 16 Map: "Through Its Eyes"

## Overview
- Grid size: 18×20
- Tileset: Circuit, data_node, void, wall, plain (metallic), fort, throne
- Terrain focus: Perfectly symmetrical grid — the System's workspace. Metallic floors, circuit-trace walls, data nodes at cardinal points. Feels artificial, designed, uncanny.

## Layout
A symmetrical arena viewed from the System's perspective. The map mirrors along both axes. The south edge (rows 16-19) is the player staging area — metallic plain tiles with two fort positions at (17, 5) and (17, 14). The center (rows 8-11, cols 6-13) is an open arena ringed by circuit tiles that grant +1 DEF. Four data_node tiles sit at cardinal points: north (4, 9), south (15, 9), east (10, 16), west (10, 2) — capturing all four weakens the Sentinel. Two ballista positions at (6, 3) and (6, 15) provide ranged siege. Walls form corridors north and south of the arena, creating two mirrored chokepoints at (12, 7) and (12, 11). The boss throne sits at (1, 9), flanked by void tiles at (1, 7) and (1, 11) that prevent flanking. Construct walls line the east and west edges.

## Tactical Notes
- **Survive then strike**: The first 12 turns are a survive phase. Test Batch enemies are weak — farm EXP but don't overextend. Save resources for Sentinel Alpha.
- **Data node capture**: Each cardinal data_node weakens the Sentinel (-2 DEF or -2 RES per node). Capturing all four strips 4 DEF and 4 RES — critical for physical and magical damage dealers. Prioritize these during the survive phase.
- **Ballista control**: Enemy constructs on ballista tiles bombard from 3-10 range. Eliminating them or seizing the ballistae (Coda or Nira) turns them into assets. Ballista damage bypasses the Sentinel's immunities.
- **Symmetrical map exploit**: The perfect symmetry means strategies that work on the left also work on the right. Split evenly or commit to one side — the System designed this map to be "fair," and the player can use that predictability.
- **Sentinel Alpha**: Immune to CRP effects. Middling SPD (10) means fast units double him. High DEF AND RES means no single damage type dominates — bring mixed offense. Orin's Dance enables burst damage from two mages in one turn.
- **Void tiles**: The void flanking the throne at (1, 7) and (1, 11) prevents side approaches. The only path to the boss is straight north through the corridor. Clearing the Construct Guards at (3, 7) and (3, 11) is mandatory before engaging.
- **System Fragment**: Drops from Sentinel Alpha on defeat. Enables Senna's anti-construct research in later chapters. Story-critical item.
