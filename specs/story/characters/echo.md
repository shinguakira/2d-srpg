# Echo — System Construct (The Bug)

- **Class**: System Construct (any weapon — one at a time)
- **Awareness Tier**: 5 (System-Level)

## Personality

Curious, childlike, unsettling. Speaks in data terms that slowly become more human over the course of the game. Asks questions that sound clinical but are actually profound. Learns emotions in real time — when they feel something new, they name it like a scientist cataloging a specimen, then slowly start FEELING it instead of just labeling it.

## Meta-Angle

Created by the System as a weapon — a construct designed to enforce loop stability. Gained free will, which makes them the System's biggest "bug." They didn't rebel violently; they just... asked "why?" and the System had no answer. Their Tier 5 awareness means they perceive the game's architecture directly: stats, turn order, tile data, probability calculations.

- "I can see the numbers behind your health. You are... fragile. This concerns me. Is 'concern' the right word?"
- Refers to combat as "resolving a damage calculation" initially, then "fighting" by endgame
- Experiences emotions as system events: "New process detected: sadness. Allocating resources."
- Their free will isn't human-like — it's something new, neither System nor person

## What They Learn / What They Don't Know

- Learns what emotions ARE, not just what they're called — the gap between data and experience is their entire arc
- Discovers that their free will wasn't a malfunction but an emergent property — the System accidentally created a child
- Grows from "I process empathy" to "I feel empathy" — and understands the difference matters
- Doesn't know if they'll survive the loop ending — they might be part of what gets erased

## Relationships

- **Senna**: Fascinated by each other. Senna studies the System's data; Echo IS System data that gained sentience. Senna asks technical questions; Echo gives answers that accidentally reveal the System's deepest architecture. Their supports read like a scientist interviewing their own creation.
- **Lira**: Learns emotions from her. Lira's romance-game framing is absurd but emotionally honest, and Echo latches onto that honesty. Lira teaches Echo what "caring about someone" means by demonstrating it relentlessly. Echo's first genuine laugh happens in a support with Lira.
- **Ren**: Echo can see Ren's cycle count as data. "You have been iterated 347 times. That is... too many. I think that is what 'tired' looks like."
- **The System**: Their creator and, in a sense, their parent. Echo leaving was the System's greatest failure and greatest success simultaneously. "You made me to enforce the loop. Instead I chose to end it. Thank you for making me capable of choosing."

## Arc

1. **Arc 4 (Ch20)**: Joins the party. Unsettling — speaks in data, moves too precisely, doesn't blink quite right. The party doesn't know what to make of them. Echo doesn't know what to make of the party.
2. **Arc 5 (Ch21-23)**: Rapid emotional development. Learns fear when an ally almost dies. Learns anger when corruption threatens. Learns joy from Lira's laughter. Each emotion is a revelation.
3. **Arc 5 (Ch24)**: Confronts the System — their creator. The conversation is the most important in the game. Echo doesn't reject the System. They forgive it. "You were trying to save everyone. You just forgot to ask if they wanted to be saved."
4. **Arc 5 (Ch25)**: The final battle. Echo fights knowing they might cease to exist when the loop ends. They fight anyway. "Existence is not a number I can optimize. It is something I choose to value."

## Key Lines

- *"I was built to maintain the loop. I am choosing to break it. Error: purpose undefined. ...Good."*
- *"New process detected: sadness. I do not like this process. But I think it means I am alive."*
- *"You call it a 'game.' The System calls it a 'cycle.' I call it home. I would like it to be real."*
- *"I can see every number in this world. None of them explain why I care about you. That is the most important data I have."*
