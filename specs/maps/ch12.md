# Chapter 12 Map: "The Turning"

## Overview
- Grid size: 16×18
- Tileset: Wall, stone floor, road, forest, shrine, void (corrupted)
- Terrain focus: Waystation monastery interior — tight corridors, shrine defense, corrupted siege

## Layout
A walled waystation compound. The south (rows 12-17) is an open courtyard where the party deploys, with a gate at row 17, cols 8-9. The central building (rows 5-11) is a rectangular monastery with walls forming corridors — a main hall (rows 7-9, cols 6-11) connects to side rooms via 1-tile-wide doorways. The northern shrine (rows 1-4, cols 7-10) is where Abbot Seras positions himself, elevated behind corrupted acolytes. A shrine tile at (2, 9) gives Seras terrain bonuses. The east and west approaches to the shrine are narrow corridors (cols 4-5 and cols 12-13, rows 3-7) — 2 tiles wide, defensible chokepoints. Corrupted tiles line the northern edge (rows 0-1, cols 0-6 and cols 11-17). Forest patches outside the walls (rows 12-15, cols 0-3 and cols 14-17) provide cover for the initial approach.

## Tactical Notes
- **Corruption loss timing**: The highest-CRP unit turns hostile on Turn 5. The player has Turns 1-4 to position non-essential units away from that party member and prepare for the betrayal. The turned unit attacks the nearest ally — control proximity.
- **Seras's light magic**: Deals bonus damage to any unit with CRP > 0. Kira (CRP 60+) takes massive extra damage from Seras and should NOT engage him directly. Send clean units (Shigeru, Corwin, Gareth) for the boss fight instead.
- **Physic healing chain**: Seras heals corrupted allies with Physic from range. If the player ignores the corrupted acolytes, Seras keeps them alive indefinitely. Either rush Seras to stop the healing, or eliminate the acolytes first to break the chain.
- **Corridor chokepoints**: The 1-tile doorways at rows 7 and 9 are critical. Position tanks (Halvar, or a promoted unit) in doorways to control enemy flow while ranged units attack from safety behind them.
- **Emotional design**: The corruption loss is the chapter's defining moment. The turned ally cannot be saved — the player must defeat their own unit. This is mechanically simple but emotionally devastating. The rest of the chapter (defeating Seras) channels that emotion into focused aggression.
- **Chest access**: The Purify staff fragment (west chest) and Talisman (east chest) require reaching side rooms through enemy-held corridors. Fenn's lockpick ability is essential. Prioritize the Purify fragment for CRP management in coming chapters.
