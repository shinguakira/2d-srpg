# Chapter 17: The Fords of Sarz

*Not yet implemented.*

## Summary

Back up the Sarz, holding the fords. The only running water between the blight and
the eastern half of the island — which means both armies want it and neither can
afford to lose it.

## Intent

- **Objective**: hold the fords for N turns
- **Mechanical payoff** of Lisette's first proven rule: the blight will not cross
  running water. The whole map is that thesis, built as terrain.
- **Lisette, part two.** She is asked to gamble the company on a rule she established
  fifteen chapters ago from a handful of soil, and has to say out loud, in front of
  everyone, whether she trusts her own work. She does. She is right. It costs her
  more to say it than to be right about it.
- **Callback**: this is the same crossing as Ch2. The bridge is still there. Halvar is
  not.
