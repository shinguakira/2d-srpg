# Chapter 14 Map: "The Monastery"

## Overview
- Grid size: 16×16
- Tileset: Monastery wall, stone floor, garden, shrine, forest, mountain, road, void (corrupted grove)
- Terrain focus: Monastery complex with library, chapel, inner sanctum — defensive layout against dark magic siege

## Layout
The monastery sits at the center of the map. The south (rows 12-15) is an open approach through forest and road — the party deploys here. The monastery proper (rows 4-11) is a walled complex: an outer cloister (rows 8-11, cols 3-12) with garden tiles and a central fountain (wall tile), connected to an inner sanctum via a main entrance at (8, 8). The library occupies the west wing (rows 5-7, cols 3-5) with a chest. The chapel fills the east wing (rows 5-7, cols 11-13) with a shrine tile at (6, 12) — Elara's ritual position. The inner sanctum (rows 4-5, cols 7-9) holds the Master Seal chest. North of the monastery (rows 0-3) is a corrupted grove — dark forest and void tiles where The Hollow positions himself at (1, 8). The grove provides terrain bonuses to the boss but is hostile to the party (CRP +5/turn on corrupted tiles).

## Tactical Notes
- **Eclipse siege pressure**: The Hollow casts Eclipse every turn from 3-10 range, targeting the highest-CRP unit. This bypasses the front line entirely — position high-CRP units (Kira, Zael) near Elara's Purify range, not in the rear where they seem safe.
- **Elara's recruitment**: Elara starts as a green NPC at the chapel shrine (6, 12). She auto-recruits when a player unit reaches her tile. Prioritize reaching her early — her Purify staves and light tomes are essential for this chapter and beyond.
- **Self-damage exploitation**: Eclipse costs The Hollow 5 HP per cast. By Turn 9, he has cast ~9 times (45 HP lost), reducing him from 55 to ~10 HP. If the party can survive the siege and reach him, he may already be near death.
- **Kira's risk-reward**: Nosferatu is effective against The Hollow but requires melee range. The corrupted grove (CRP +5/turn) means Kira takes corruption damage while fighting. Use her for a single burst attack, then pull back. Do not leave her in the grove.
- **Light magic debut**: Elara's light tomes reduce CRP on corrupted enemies and deal bonus damage. This chapter teaches the player that light magic is the corruption counter — a lesson that pays off for the rest of the game.
- **Master Seal timing**: The chest at (4, 8) contains a Master Seal. Combined with the Hero Crest from Ch7 (if obtained), the player now has two promotion opportunities. Promote Kanna or a frontliner based on party needs.
