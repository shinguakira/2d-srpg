## Bosses

### Ch1 — Garrek the Bandit Chief
- **Class**: Fighter (axe)
- **AI**: Aggressive
- **Awareness Tier**: 1 (Full — remembers cycles)
- **Personality**: Knows he's the tutorial boss. Has been killed 347 times. More tired than Ren. Started writing a speech in cycle #50, has refined it over 300 cycles, and Ren STILL won't let him finish it.
- **Key line**: *"I am Garrek the— stop YAWNING. This is my INTRO SPEECH."*
- **Secret moment**: Drops the act post-combat. Quiet, raw: "You remember too, don't you?" — the first hint that awareness exists outside the protagonist.

### Ch2 — Commander Thane
- **Class**: Cavalier (lance)
- **AI**: Guard (radius)
- **Awareness Tier**: 4 (Unaware)
- **Personality**: Professional military commander. Follows orders. Thinks "cycles" and "seeds" are enemy code words. His normalcy is both the joke and the tragedy.
- **Key line**: *"I don't know what a 'save file' is. I know my duty."*
- **Role**: Straight man. Demonstrates that the reset WORKS on most people — Thane is exactly who the System intended everyone to be. His sincerity makes the meta characters' existential dread feel earned.

### Ch3 — General Morryn
- **Class**: Soldier (lance, armored)
- **AI**: Boss (stays on throne)
- **Awareness Tier**: 2 (Partial — feels echoes)
- **Personality**: Just beginning to crack. Feels déjà vu that won't stop. Half-remembers dying on this throne. Can't tell if the memories are real or madness. Lashing out because the uncertainty is worse than the truth.
- **Key line**: *"I remember dying. I remember it happening AGAIN. Make it stop."*
- **Role**: Mirror of what Ren could have become if he'd broken instead of going numb. First boss that earns genuine emotion from the player. His request to Ren — "make it stop" — is both a combat invitation and a mercy plea.

## Generic Enemies — Awareness by Type

Not every enemy talks. Only 1-2 per chapter get dialogue for comedic/dramatic beats. Most are just units.

### Soldiers (Tier 4 — Unaware)
Follow orders without question. The most "normal" enemies. Exist to be fought.
- Occasional beat: "For the commander!" (they genuinely mean it)

### Brigands (Tier 4 — Unaware, but bitter)
Know they're weak. Don't know WHY they feel disposable. Some have vague resentment.
- Occasional beat: "Why do I always spawn near the strongest enemy...?"

### Mages (Tier 2 — Partially Aware)
High MAG stat resonance means enemy mages sometimes perceive the system layer.
- Occasional beat: "I KNOW I should retreat, but something compels me forward." (describing their AI behavior without understanding it)

### Archers (Tier 4 — Unaware)
Defined entirely by their 2-range. It's their whole personality. Blissfully ignorant.
- Occasional beat: "You can't reach me from there!" (proud, genuine)

### Reinforcements (Tier 4 — Disoriented)
Spawned mid-battle. Arrive confused. Don't know where they came from.
- Occasional beat: "Wait, when did this battle START? ...Why am I already fighting?"
