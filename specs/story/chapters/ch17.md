# Chapter 17: River of Regret

*Not yet implemented.*

## Summary

A river crossing under fire, on the only water the blight will not cross for fifty
miles — which means both armies need it and neither can afford to lose it.

## Intent

- **Objective**: hold the fords for N turns
- **Mechanical payoff** of Kanna's first proven rule: running water stops the blight.
  The whole map is that thesis, built as terrain.
- **Kanna, part two.** She is asked to gamble the company on a rule she established
  six chapters ago from a handful of soil, and has to say out loud, in front of
  everyone, whether she trusts her own work. She does. She is right. It costs her
  more to say it than to be right about it.
