# Chapter 21: The Forbidden Ground

*Arc 5 opener. Not yet implemented.*

## Summary

**The Dreadful Place** — at the heart of Taragi's uncut
forest. Ground no layman may set foot on, and the party has to walk into it.

## Intent

- **Objective**: reach the stone at the centre
- **Rule the map is built on**: the forbidden ground is Hallowed Ground. The blight
  will not cross it. It is the only safe footing on the mountain, and it is also the
  one place everyone in the company has been raised to believe they must not stand.
- **Mirelle carries this chapter.** She is the only one entitled to be there and she is
  the one most frightened of it.
- **The plan becomes sayable**: two flames burn, the third can be relit, and the
  Flamebrand is the fuel. What remains is reaching Arn through the Emperor's guard.
- **Weight**: Shigeru states the cost to the company and takes a vote he does not
  need and is not obliged to take. The deliberate inverse of Ch8, where he made the
  call alone and has been carrying it since.
