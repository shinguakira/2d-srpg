# Chapter 3: "Scout's Honor"

## Summary

First chapter with the full 6-unit party. Introduces terrain variety — forests, hills, forts — as a tactical tool rather than decoration. The party assaults a hilltop fortress held by Captain Holtz, a professional soldier with no meta-awareness and no nonsense. Along the way, they rescue Nira, an archer defending a village alone. First glitched tiles appear on the map. The comedy stays sharp, but the environment itself is starting to crack.

## Prologue Dialogue

**Scene**: Rolling hills ahead. Forests cluster between ridgelines. A fortress sits on the highest hill, banner flying. Two villages visible in the valleys.

- **Kael**: "Open ground! Finally. Room to charge!"
- **Ren**: "Don't go too far ahead. There's a village under attack in the eastern valley."
- **Kael**: "How do you know that?"
- **Ren**: "...Smoke. I see smoke."
- **Senna**: "I don't see any smoke."
- **Ren**: "I have good eyes."

- **Senna**: "Movement cost varies by terrain type. Forests are 2, plains are 1, mountains are—"
- **Bram**: "I don't want a LECTURE. I want to PUNCH things."
- **Lira**: "Maybe the trees are a metaphor? For obstacles in relationships?"
- **Ren**: "They're trees, Lira. Let's move."

## Mid-Battle Events

### Turn 2 — Terrain Matters

Bram charges forward across open ground and gets peppered by an archer on a hill. Meanwhile, Voss holds a forest tile and tanks a hit easily.

- **Bram**: "OW. How did that archer hit me from way over there??"
- **Senna**: "You're standing on a plain. Zero avoid bonus. If you'd stopped in that forest tile one step back, you'd have had +20 avoid."
- **Bram**: "I'm not hiding behind a TREE."
- **Voss**: *(from a forest tile, enemy misses him)* "I'm hiding behind a tree. Very comfortable."
- **Kael**: "The fortress ahead has fort tiles — those give even more defense. The boss will be on one."
- **Ren**: "Kael's right. We can't brute force a fort tile. We'll need Senna's magic or weapon advantage."

### Turn 3-4 — Nira's Rescue (Village Visit)

Player approaches the eastern village tile. A lone archer stands on the elevated hill beside the village, methodically picking off bandits as they approach. She's outnumbered but hasn't missed a shot.

- **Kael**: "There's someone defending that village alone!"
- **Ren**: "An archer. On the high ground."
- *(Player visits village tile)*
- **Nira**: *(doesn't look at them, fires another arrow, bandit drops)* "Took you long enough."
- **Ren**: "You held them off by yourself?"
- **Nira**: "Bow. High ground. They can't reach me. Simple."
- **Lira**: "Oh! A mysterious loner with a bow! Are you the stoic rival type or the—"
- **Nira**: "I'm a hunter."
- **Lira**: "...The strong-silent type. Got it."
- **Bram**: "Can she DO that? Just stand there and shoot people who can't hit back?"
- **Senna**: "Bows have 2-range. She can hit enemies two tiles away, but she can't fight at melee range. No counterattacks up close."
- **Nira**: "So don't let them get close. That's my only rule."

**[Nira joins the party. Receives Iron Bow. Sightbow (Prf, personal weapon) already equipped.]**

### Turn 5-6 — The First Crack

A forest tile at the edge of the map flickers. For a single frame, it becomes a plain tile, then snaps back. No gameplay effect — just visual.

- **Senna**: "Did that tile just... change?"
- **Ren**: "What tile?"
- **Senna**: "That forest. It was a plain for a second. The terrain data stuttered."
- **Voss**: "Terrain doesn't stutter."
- **Senna**: "I know. That's why I'm concerned."
- **Ren**: *(quietly)* "...Don't worry about it. Not yet."
- **Senna**: "Ren, if the map data is unstable—"
- **Ren**: "I said not yet. Focus on Holtz."

*(Senna gives Ren a long look but drops it.)*

## Boss Fight — Captain Holtz

Holtz stands on a fort tile at the hilltop fortress entrance. Guard AI — he won't leave his 3-tile radius. High DEF. Lance equipped. The fort tile gives him +3 DEF and +10 Avoid on top of his already solid defense.

- **Pre-combat**:
  - **Holtz**: "This fortress will not fall while I draw breath."
  - **Ren**: "Captain Holtz. You don't have to die here."
  - **Holtz**: "I received my orders this morning. Hold the fort. Repel all attackers. I don't know who you are and I don't care."
  - **Ren**: "You received those orders 347 mornings ago."
  - **Holtz**: "...I don't understand what that means. And I don't need to. Raise your weapon."

**Mechanical note**: Holtz's high DEF + fort tile bonus means physical attacks deal minimal damage. Senna's magic ignores DEF. Alternatively, Nira's bow can chip from safe range. This teaches players that brute force doesn't work against fortified armored bosses — plan around the defense.

- **Post-combat** (after defeat):
  - **Holtz**: "The fort... falls..."
  - **Kael**: "He fought well. Just a soldier doing his duty."
  - **Ren**: "Yeah. Most of them are."
  - **Nira**: "Was there another way? Without killing him?"
  - **Ren**: "...Not in this version."

## Story Beats Delivered

- Terrain as tactical tool — forest cover, fort bonuses, elevated positions all demonstrated through gameplay and dialogue
- Nira joins — bow mechanics introduced (2-range only, can't counter melee, positional gameplay)
- Captain Holtz as straight military boss — no meta-awareness, no jokes, just duty (contrasts Garrek's full awareness in Ch1)
- First glitched tile — subtle, no gameplay impact yet, but Senna notices (System escalation: dormant → first cracks)
- Ren's foreknowledge shown again — he knows about the village, about the glitches, about what comes next
- Party dynamics expanding — Nira's quiet pragmatism vs Lira's social energy, Bram's frustration with terrain rules
- Teaching moment: fortified bosses require strategy (magic, weapon advantage) not raw damage
- Setup for Ch4: thieves in the next town (Coda's recruitment chapter)
