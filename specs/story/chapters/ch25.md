# Final: The Shrine at Arn

***Built and playable.*** Full profile in [bosses.md](../bosses.md).

## Summary

**Arn.** The shrine on the cliff above the natural arch, the oldest in the realm,
raised over the place where the Blackflame came ashore four centuries ago and the
347 stopped it. Takeshi is waiting on the shrine floor.

The argument, and then the fight.

## Arn is a valley, not a rock in the sea

**The board is the settlement, not the arch.** Arn is 阿連 — a place on the west
coast where a river comes down to the sea, with a strip of flat ground, terraces,
and mountains on three sides. The sea is the **west edge only**; everything else
is land, and the party walks in from the coast road at the south, out of the hole
they cut in Ch24.

The sea arch itself is offshore, outside the frame. It is heard — the tide going
through it, on turn 5 — and seen from the cliff in the epilogue, and it is where
the seal is. It is not a tile. Drawing the finale as a rock surrounded by open
ocean puts the shrine somewhere Arn is not.

What the valley gives the fight: the **river** across its middle with only two
bridges, so the approach is a choice made twice; the **precinct** (`H`) on the
high ground at the north-west, where blight cannot follow; and the mountains on
the east, which only fliers can use. There is no retreat, but the reason is the
clock and the ground behind, not a cliff on four sides.

## Intent

- **Objective**: defeat Takeshi
- **Boss**: Takeshi, carrying the Blackflame in his own body. Multi-phase — the man
  first, then progressively less of the man.
- **The map is the story**: the shrine precinct is Hallowed Ground, the river cuts
  the valley in half with two bridges over it, and the seal is out under the arch
  offshore. There is nowhere to retreat to and both of them know it.

## Writing rules for the finale

- **Takeshi never concedes the argument.** He is not talked down by being proved
  wrong, because he is not wrong about the thing he is right about: the seal was a
  delay, and it has already failed once.
- **Shigeru does not win the debate. He refuses it.** His position is that a delay
  bought four centuries, that four centuries is four centuries, and that Takeshi's
  alternative requires one man to stay certain forever.
- **The best outcome is a choice, not a defeat** (`takeshiSpared`). The player earns
  the chance to make Takeshi put it down by having spared people all campaign — the
  ending flags are the receipts for that, not arbitrary collectibles.
- **The cost is fixed and known since Ch19.** Relighting the seal spends all 347
  embers. The Flamebrand goes dark on the shrine floor and Shigeru leaves it there.
  Amagi's kings do not carry it again, and that is the correct ending for it.
- **The last shot** should be the sea arch with the tide going through it, from the
  cliff where Ch1's prologue said the king rode west and did not come back.
