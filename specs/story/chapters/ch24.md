# Chapter 24: The Northern Shrine

*Not yet implemented.*

## Summary

The broken shrine itself: the seal, the ward Takeshi cracked eleven months ago, and
the last approach.

## Intent

- **Objective**: seize the shrine floor
- **Mechanics**: the map at its most hostile — rifts, Colossi, and terrain that
  changes between turns. Hallowed Ground is the only safe footing and there is not
  enough of it, which turns the whole chapter into a positioning problem rather than
  a damage race.
- **Set-up for the finale**: relighting the seal becomes a physical thing the player
  can point at — an unlit brazier on the shrine floor, and a sword with 347 embers
  in it. No further explanation should be needed by the time Ch25 starts.
