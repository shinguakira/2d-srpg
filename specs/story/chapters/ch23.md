# Chapter 23: The Oath of Three Hundred

*Not yet implemented.*

## Summary

Under the Dreadful Place. The chamber where the 347 burned, and the wall their names are
carved into.

## Intent

- **Objective**: survive / advance through the seal chamber
- **Lore climax**: the oath was not what the songs say. They did not defeat the
  Blackflame. They agreed to hold a door with their own bodies, and they expected to
  be relieved. Nobody ever came.
- **Consequence**: this reframes Takeshi's whole position as a reasonable reading of
  the evidence rather than a madman's. He is not denying the sacrifice — he watched
  what it bought and refuses to ask anyone to repeat it.
- **Shigeru reads the wall.** Three hundred and forty-seven names, and one of them is
  the reason his family has carried a sword for four hundred years.
- **Set-up for Ch24.** Once the oath is *holding a door* rather than *winning a
  fight*, the Stone Gate on the Arn road stops being a closed shortcut and
  becomes the outer half of that door. The player should work that out one
  chapter before the party has to break it open —— see
  [ch24.md](ch24.md) and [world.md](../world.md#two-doors-not-one).
