# Chapter 24: The Cliffs of Are

*Not yet implemented.*

## Summary

The last approach: four miles of the west coast between Komoda and Are, sheer cliff
on one side and the western sea on the other, on a path wide enough for two.

## Intent

- **Objective**: advance to the Are river mouth
- **Terrain**: the most constrained map in the game. A cliff road, a handful of
  ledges, the sea below, and Abyssal Rifts opening in the path itself. Fliers matter
  enormously here and are also the most exposed — the payoff for keeping Yuki and
  Tsubame alive.
- **Escalation**: the ground is at its most hostile. Rifts, Colossi, and terrain that
  changes between turns. Hallowed Ground is the only safe footing and there is not
  enough of it, which turns the chapter into a positioning problem rather than a
  damage race.
- **Set-up for the finale**: the party comes around the last headland and sees it —
  the sea arch, the shrine on the cliff above it, and the seal underneath. Relighting
  it becomes a physical thing the player can point at: an unlit brazier on the shrine
  floor, and a sword with 347 embers in it.
