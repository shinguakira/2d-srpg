# Chapter 16: The Mad Governor

*Arc 4 opener. Not yet implemented.*

## Summary

Into Kurogane's own provinces, where a governor has kept the grey out of the
capital's sight for a year by the simple method of not filing the reports.

## Intent

- **Objective**: seize
- **The reveal**: the Empire's back country is going grey behind its own front line,
  and its officials have all made the same choice Tetsuzan, Hyodo and Isonami made —
  hold the post, do not look north. The pattern the player has been meeting since
  Ch3 turns out to go all the way to the top.
- **Boss**: the governor, who is not mad at all. He has been buying his province time
  by lying to his emperor, and he is the first enemy in the campaign who begs the
  party to keep going after he loses.
