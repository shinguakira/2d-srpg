# Chapter 13: The Uzia Basin

***Built and playable.***

## Summary

The **Uzia basin**, the one wide valley inland, ringed by Yatan, Maishi and
Taragi. A crossing under a wyvern ambush from the ridges, and the party's first
recruit who is already carrying the blight.

## Intent

- **Objective**: seize the far bridgehead
- **New mechanic**: verticality — fliers holding ledges, bows as the answer
- **Joins**: a wyvern rider who is already carrying the blight. A conditional
  recruit and a standing risk: leave them standing in the grey long enough and
  they turn mid-battle, permanently, and the counter is on screen from the moment
  they join. No hidden trap.
- **Boss**: a Kurogane sniper captain holding the Maishi ridge, left behind by her own
  army's retreat, who has decided the gorge is hers now.
