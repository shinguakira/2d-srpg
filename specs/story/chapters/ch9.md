# Chapter 9: The Empty Place

Not implemented.

## Summary

The grief chapter. A narrow forest pass, an ordinary rout, and a party that is
mechanically weaker because of it — every unit carries the `grief` trauma skill
(-3 stats) for this chapter and the next. Viviane joins to give the arc a
counterweight that is not a joke at grief's expense.

## Setup

- **Map**: 14×14, linear forest pass, forts at (4,4)/(9,4)/(4,8)/(9,8)
- **Objective**: Rout
- **Deploy**: 7 slots, `shigeru` forced
- **Enemies**: an anonymous raider captain and eight scouts — deliberately not
  characters
- **Joins**: `viviane`, turn 3
- **Par**: 18 turns

## Beats

**Prologue.** Nobody has taken the empty place in the marching order. Gareth's line is
a single ellipsis and should stay that way. Akira: *"He would have taken the
rearguard. He always took the rearguard, and he never once made a thing of it."*

**Turn 1.** The left flank is open. Bryn names who would have filled it; Corwin takes
it and is honest that he is not the same — *"I am not as stubborn as he was, but I
will hold."*

**Turn 3 — Viviane.** Steps out of the treeline expecting applause. She has been
waiting two days for somebody interesting to walk past, and diagnoses the company on
sight: *"you lot look like a funeral that has not finished walking to the grave."*
She attaches herself over Shigeru's objection.

**Turn 4 — Dance tutorial.** Lisette frames it mechanically and in-fiction at once: a
dancer can rouse a spent soldier to move again, which given the state of this
company is worth more than another sword.

**Turn 5 — grief.** Two lines, no speeches. Akira keeps checking the rear; he knows
what is there and keeps checking it. Mirelle: *"So do I."*

**Epilogue.** Viviane's mask slips exactly once. Told they are not a travelling
company: *"No. You are not. You are something that lost its heart and kept walking
anyway."* Then, quietly, that she has seen that look before on better people than
her — it does not go away, but it gets quieter — and she never mentions it again.

Lisette closes on the mechanical truth: everyone is still carrying it, they will not
be themselves for another engagement, and it is not a thing she can fix with a chart.

## Supports

- **Shigeru × Viviane** — she reads him as a man in the third act and asks for the
  terrible secret. He says there is none. She does not believe him, and is right.
- **Gareth × Corwin** — where does a sellsword learn to fight that tidy? Twenty years,
  most of them for people he did not like.
