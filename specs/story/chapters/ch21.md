# Chapter 21: The Three Flames

*Arc 5 opener. Not yet implemented.*

## Summary

The plan is finally sayable out loud: two flames still burn, the third can be
relit, and the Flamebrand is the fuel. What remains is reaching the northern shrine
through the Emperor's personal guard.

## Intent

- **Objective**: seize the mountain gate
- **Structure**: the war-council chapter — the full surviving roster and every ally
  earned since Ch14 on one map.
- **Weight**: Shigeru states the cost to the company and takes a vote he does not
  need and is not obliged to take. It is the deliberate inverse of Ch8, where he made
  the call alone and has been carrying it since.
