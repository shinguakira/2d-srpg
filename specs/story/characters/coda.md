# Coda — Thief (The Data Extractor)

- **Class**: Thief (knives)
- **Awareness Tier**: 3 (Genre-Displaced — thinks stealth game)

## Personality

Mischievous, fast-talking, kleptomaniac. Never uses one word when twelve will do. Speaks like a heist movie narrator providing running commentary on their own life. Collects "interesting data" from everyone and everything — pockets full of stolen trinkets they insist are "intel."

## Meta-Angle

Thinks they're in a stealth game. Combat is "getting spotted." Stealing is "data extraction." Allies are "the crew." Every mission is a heist, every chapter a new infiltration zone. Their genre displacement is comedic but also weirdly useful — they actually think about stealth and positioning more than anyone else.

- Refers to enemies as "hostiles" and combat as "loud mode"
- Calls treasure chests "data caches" and lockpicking "decryption"
- "Going loud" is their term for getting drawn into open combat
- Genuinely confused why the party keeps "triggering alarms" (starting fights)

## What They Learn / What They Don't Know

- Learns that their "data extraction" is actually revealing real truths about the System's architecture
- Never fully abandons the stealth-game framing, but starts using it as a lens for genuine insight
- Discovers that the corruption has "encrypted zones" they can navigate better than anyone
- Doesn't realize their collected "data" is actually pieces of the System's source code

## Relationships

- **Lira**: Both genre-displaced, opposite energy. Lira sees romance everywhere; Coda sees stealth missions. They bond over being the only two who "get" that everyone else is playing the wrong game. Their supports are hilarious.
- **Nira**: Nira disapproves of the stealing. Coda keeps leaving stolen trinkets in Nira's quiver as "gifts." Nira keeps returning them. Neither will stop.
- **Senna**: Coda's stolen "data" occasionally contains genuinely useful System fragments. Senna is torn between disapproval and fascination.
- **Bram**: Coda calls Bram "the distraction." Bram doesn't mind. It's the most effective tactical partnership in the party.

## Arc

1. **Arc 1 (Ch4-5)**: Joins mid-heist. Was stealing from the same enemies the party is fighting. "Oh, you're the distraction team? Perfect." Immediately integrates because their skillset fills a gap.
2. **Arc 2 (Ch6-10)**: Their "data extraction" starts producing real results — stolen items contain System fragments. Coda doesn't understand what they've found but Senna does.
3. **Arc 3 (Ch11-15)**: Navigates corrupted zones better than anyone because they approach it as "encrypted terrain." Their stealth-game thinking becomes genuinely strategic.
4. **Arc 4-5 (Ch16-25)**: Realizes their "game" was closer to reality than anyone thought. The System IS a kind of program. Coda was the only one thinking in the right terms all along.

## Key Lines

- *"I don't steal. I extract data from insecure containers."*
- *"We went loud again. You people are TERRIBLE at stealth."*
- *"Wait — this data is real? I've been right this whole time and nobody LISTENED?"*
- *"Every lock is just a question. I'm very good at questions."*
