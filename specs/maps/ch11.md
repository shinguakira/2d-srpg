# Chapter 11 Map: "Spreading Plague"

## Overview
- Grid size: 18×18
- Tileset: Road, village, forest, plain, wall, void (corrupted/glitched), mountain
- Terrain focus: Corrupted market town — glitched terrain patches spread each turn, NPC escape routes

## Layout
A square market town. The town center (rows 7-10, cols 7-10) is a marketplace with road tiles and two village tiles for civilian interaction. Residential blocks (wall tiles) divide the map into quadrants. The east gate exit is at (9, 17) and the south gate exit is at (17, 9) — these are the NPC escape destinations. The west side (cols 0-4) is already partially corrupted at start: 4 glitched tiles at rows 3-4, cols 1-2. More corruption spreads from these tiles each turn, moving east. The north edge (rows 0-2) has mountain/forest terrain separating the corrupted zone from a northern approach path. Five NPC villagers are scattered across the map. The Corrupted General roams from a starting position at (3, 3) in the corrupted zone.

## Tactical Notes
- **Dynamic terrain**: Corruption spreads 2 tiles per turn from Turn 2 onward. The playable map shrinks over time — the party must move east and south to stay on safe ground. Standing on glitched tiles costs CRP +5/turn, which can push Kira (CRP 60) or Gareth toward dangerous thresholds.
- **Villager escort priority**: Five NPCs flee toward exits but their pathing is imperfect. The panicking villager on Turn 7 reverses course — Elin's flying speed is essential for the intercept. Saving 3 of 5 is required; saving all 5 is possible but demands near-perfect positioning.
- **Kira's Nosferatu**: Kira joins at CRP 60 with Nosferatu — HP drain on hit. She's the most effective anti-corruption fighter, but her high CRP means she cannot stand on glitched tiles. Keep her on clean terrain and send her against corrupted enemies at range.
- **Corrupted General's randomization**: Stats shift ±3 each turn. Check his DEF before committing — a low-DEF turn is the window for physical attackers. A high-DEF turn means switch to magic. His erratic AI may cause him to heal instead of attack, giving the party a free turn.
- **Two-exit design**: East gate (9, 17) and south gate (17, 9) split the NPC routes. Position escort units near both exits. Fenn's high SPD makes them an excellent villager shepherd for the east gate.
- **CRP management debut**: This is the first chapter where CRP is a persistent battlefield threat. Track exposure carefully — Gareth (frontliner) is most at risk from glitched tiles during the push west toward the boss.
