# Chapter 22 Map: "Ghosts of Cycles Past"

## Overview
- Grid size: 22×22
- Tileset: Archive_tile, wall, plain (library floor), chest, void, circuit, throne, fort
- Terrain focus: A vast archive library. Shelves form walls. Phantom enemies spawn from stored memories. The map echoes Ch1's layout — distorted and expanded. The Archivist sits on the original throne position.

## Layout
A massive library mirroring Ch1's layout at triple scale with distortions. The south (rows 18-21) is the entrance hall — open library floor with archive_tile shelving along the walls. The center (rows 8-17) is the main archive: rows of wall segments (bookshelves) creating 3-tile-wide aisles running north-south at cols 3, 7, 11, 15, 19. Gaps between shelves allow east-west movement at rows 10 and 14. The northwest (rows 4-7, cols 0-7) holds the phantom spawn zone — open space where ghost enemies materialize. The northeast (rows 4-7, cols 14-21) holds the archive chest at (5, 19), behind a time-locked mechanism. The Archivist sits on a throne at (1, 11) — the same relative position as Ch1's boss. A circuit-trace ring at rows 2-3, cols 8-14 surrounds the throne. Ghost Baraku's echo appears at (6, 4). Phantom Akira appears at (9, 11) on Turn 8.

## Tactical Notes
- **Weakness rotation**: The Archivist cycles weaknesses every turn: fire (red aura) → thunder (yellow) → wind (green) → physical (white). Hitting with the correct type deals double damage. Wrong type deals half. Coordinate units to match the rotation.
- **Master Crown heist**: The archive chest at (5, 19) locks 2 turns after the Turn 6 announcement. Hachi must reach it by Turn 8. Pre-position Hachi on the right side of the map. Kagura's Dance can give Hachi extra movement range if needed.
- **Phantom waves**: Waves spawn every 3 turns with increasing strength. Early phantoms (Lv 10-12) are trivial. Late phantoms (Lv 18-22) are dangerous. Assign 2-3 units to phantom duty while the rest focus the boss.
- **Baraku's hint**: Ghost Baraku appears at Turn 4 and reveals the weakness rotation. If you miss the dialogue, watch the aura color: red/yellow/green/white maps to fire/thunder/wind/physical.
- **Akira's Pride**: Phantom Akira at Turn 8 buffs adjacent units with +3 all stats for 3 turns. Position strong units near (9, 11) before Turn 8 to maximize the buff for the final push.
- **Shelf navigation**: Wall segments (bookshelves) create aisles. Ground units must follow aisles; Yuki flies over them. The gaps at rows 10 and 14 allow east-west movement — use these for repositioning.
