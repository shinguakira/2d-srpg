# Arc Structure — 25 Chapters

Five arcs of five chapters, all of them in the **Lower Country** of Amagi. The
campaign runs two clocks: the **war** with Kurogane (fast, chapter-scale, winnable)
and the **blight** creeping inland from the broken seal at **Are** on the west coast
(slow, campaign-scale, not winnable by fighting). Arc 1 is the first. Arc 2 is
discovering the second. Arcs 3-5 are walking west to end it.

The **Upper Country**, across the Cut, is optional post-campaign content. The story
never crosses the channel.

---

## Arc Overview

| Arc | Chapters | Title | Where | Tone |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1-5 | **Flight** | East coast → Sasu valley → Tsutsu → Kaneda | Grim but bright; a company forming |
| 2 | 6-10 | **The Broken Seal** | Aso Bay → the Cut → Yatate → Shiratake → Komoda | Darkening; grief |
| 3 | 11-15 | **The Ash Road** | Inland, into the country the blight has taken | Bleak, endurance |
| 4 | 16-20 | **The Empire's Back** | Kurogane-held ground going grey behind the front | Bittersweet, political |
| 5 | 21-25 | **The Three Flames** | Tateragyama → the west cliffs → **Are** | Earned catharsis |

## The route

The campaign is a circuit of the Lower Country that ends where the story began —
at the western cliffs.

```
        Aso Bay ─── the Cut (7)
       /   |   \
 Kaneda(5) |    Kechi(6)
     |   Shiratake(9)
     |     |
  ARE(25) Yatate(8) ── Izuhara (falls before ch1)
   \       |                |
 Komoda(10)|              Kuta(1)
     \  Sasu(2) ── Shiine(3)
      \    |
       Uchiyama(13) ── Tateragyama(21-23)
              \
              Tsutsu(4)
```

## The Blight Clock

Lisette's running count is the spine of Arcs 1-2. Each mark is one chapter's worth of
evidence, and the player should be able to follow the deduction without being told:

| Chapter | Mark | What Lisette concludes |
|---|---|---|
| Ch2 | Grey grass at the Sasu bridge, in a straight line | "Something drew that." |
| Ch3 | A grey stand of pines on the same bearing | "Two marks make a line. Three would make it a road." |
| Ch4 | A Tsutsu back lane no cat will walk, same bearing | (She walks it at night and does not say what she found.) |
| Ch5 | A hole in the sky over the western cliffs, same bearing; a rift *moving toward Shigeru* | "It went past two towns to get here. It wants something we are carrying." |
| Ch6 | The Sasu valley is gone; the blight passed them in the night | It is no longer ahead of them — it is around them. |
| Ch7 | It starts moving to stay ahead of her readings | Not weather. It notices being measured. |
| Ch10 | A Colossus comes for the chronicle, then its ash runs west against the wind | Whatever built it wants its pieces back, and that leads to the door. |

Every bearing in Arc 1 is **west-north-west** — the line from wherever the party is
standing to the shrine at Are.

## Arc 1 — Flight (Ch1-5)

Kurogane lands at Komoda; the king dies on the sand; Izuhara burns. The company is
assembled on the run down the east coast and then west across the island.

- **Ch1 — The Road to Kuta.** The coast-road gate keep south of Izuhara, held by
  brigands on Kurogane pay. Boss: Hagen. Joins: Gareth (turn 2), Mirelle (epilogue).
- **Ch2 — The Sasu Crossing.** The garrison on the only bridge for thirty miles.
  Boss: Commander Vidar. Joins: Halvar (turn 3, defects). Vidar carries a **cracked
  shrine ward from Are** — the first hard evidence.
- **Ch3 — The Shiine Hills.** Brigands in the Sasu uplands. Boss: Captain Olrik.
  Joins: Bryn. Terrain tutorial; first blight underfoot.
- **Ch4 — The Cape of Tsutsu.** The southern cape's last free harbour, three
  storehouses to hold. Boss: Brask. Joins: Fenn. Brask carries a Kurogane pay chit.
- **Ch5 — Kaneda, Above the Clouds.** The old border fortress on Shiroyama above Aso
  Bay, built four centuries ago to watch the western sea. Boss: General Roderic.
  Joins: Elin. **Arc turn:** the rift moves toward Shigeru, and Halvar names Takeshi
  and Are out loud. The company turns west.

## Arc 2 — The Broken Seal (Ch6-10)

- **Ch6 — The Harbour at Kechi.** Aso Bay's inner water. Boss: Captain Aeryn.
  Joins: Corwin, Nadine. The blight has passed them; every road south is cut.
- **Ch7 — What the Wall Held.** The fortress at the Cut. Survive 12 turns while
  revenants come out of the seaward wall. Optional boss: Admiral Varro.
  **Lisette's crisis chapter.**
- **Ch8 — The Last Stand on Yatate.** The fortress on the island's highest ground,
  two fronts. Boss: General Wulfram. **Halvar dies** holding the lower corridor for
  nine turns, by his own choice and against a direct order. Grief for two chapters.
- **Ch9 — The Empty Place.** The uncut forest under Shiratake. Joins: Viviane.
- **Ch10 — The Sands of Komoda.** The invasion beach, one arc later: village below,
  fortified hill above. Boss: Grand Magus Ezrin. A **Blackflame Colossus** spawns on
  turn 6 and goes for Elder Ilse's chronicle. **Arc close: Takeshi appears in
  person**, unarmed, and asks for the Flamebrand. He is refused.

## Arc 3 — The Ash Road (Ch11-15)

Inland, up the Sasu, into the country the blight has already taken. Grey villages,
rifts, revenants in Amagi colours. The party learns the rules the hard way: running
water stops it, hallowed ground pushes it back. Ends with the southern Sacred Flame
at Tsutsu going out.

## Arc 4 — The Empire's Back (Ch16-20)

Kurogane-held ground is going grey behind its own front line and its officers know.
The moral case against Takeshi stops being Amagi's and becomes his own army's. Ends
in the uncut wood under Tateragyama.

## Arc 5 — The Three Flames (Ch21-25)

- **Ch21-23 — Tateragyama.** The Osoroshidokoro, the forbidden ground, and the wall
  the 347 names are carved into.
- **Ch24 — The Cliffs of Are.** The last approach along the west coast.
- **Ch25 — Final: The Sea Gate.** The shrine above the natural arch at Are, over the
  seal itself. Takeshi never concedes the argument; the best outcome is his choosing
  to put it down.

---

## Post-campaign — the Upper Country

North across the Cut: Mitake, the cold coasts, the watchtowers that look at the
enemy shore. Optional maps at higher difficulty, no story dependency, and nothing
there that the campaign requires the player to have seen.

---

## Endings

Evaluated in a scoring pass that does not exist yet.

| Ending | Condition | Title |
|---|---|---|
| Perfect | Seal relit + Blackflame ended + 0 deaths + Aeryn recruited + shrine keeper saved + **Takeshi spared** | *The Fourth Flame* |
| True | Seal relit + Blackflame ended | *The Seal Restored* |
| Bittersweet | Blackflame ended, seal never relit | *Ash and Aftermath* |
| Tragic | 5 or more deaths | *What It Took* |

Relighting the seal at Are spends all 347 embers of the Flamebrand. The sword goes
dark and Shigeru leaves it on the shrine floor.
