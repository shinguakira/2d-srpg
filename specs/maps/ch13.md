# Chapter 13 Map: "Second Chances"

## Overview
- Grid size: 18×20
- Tileset: Mountain, forest, fortress wall, stone floor, road, plain, fort, gate
- Terrain focus: Highland fortress with elevated wyvern perches, mountain approaches, fortress gate objective

## Layout
A vertical map ascending toward a highland fortress. The south (rows 15-19) is a mountain pass — narrow road (cols 8-11) flanked by impassable mountain. The mid-section (rows 8-14) opens into a rocky plateau with scattered forest and two elevated perch positions (mountain-adjacent fort tiles at (10, 4) and (10, 15)) where Zael and his wyvern circle. The fortress occupies the north (rows 0-7): outer walls at rows 6-7, an inner courtyard (rows 3-5, cols 6-13), and the fortress gate (seize tile) at (1, 10). The west approach (cols 2-5, rows 4-8) winds through heavy forest — slower but avoids the fortified front. The east approach (cols 14-17, rows 4-8) is an exposed cliff path with mountain tiles providing cover but no forest. Two chokepoints guard the gate: the main entrance at (6, 9-10) and a side door at (6, 5).

## Tactical Notes
- **Zael recruitment window**: Zael must be at 5 HP or less when Shigeru uses Talk. Over-damage kills him. His CRP-induced stat shifts (±2) make damage forecasts unreliable — use weak attacks to chip him down carefully. Bryn's bow at range is ideal for precise HP management.
- **CRP ticking clock**: Zael's CRP starts at 43 (after Turn 2 combat) and ticks up. If it reaches 60 before Shigeru talks to him, the window closes. Viviane's Dance on Shigeru can give the extra movement needed to reach Zael's retreat position on the fortress roof at (2, 7).
- **Two boss paths**: If Zael is recruited, the Fortress Warden is the boss — a standard high-DEF General fight. If Zael is killed or escapes, Zael himself replaces The Hollow as the Ch14 boss. The recruitment path is harder short-term but better long-term.
- **Elevated terrain**: The fort perches at (10, 4) and (10, 15) give +2 DEF/+5 Avoid. Elin can land on these for a defensible aerial position. Ground units must approach through the plateau.
- **Three approaches**: Main gate (6, 9-10) is heavily defended. Side door (6, 5) has an archer overwatch. West forest path is slow but provides cover. Splitting forces across two approaches and flanking the gate from inside is the advanced strategy.
- **Killer Lance chest**: The chest at (3, 15) contains a Killer Lance. If Zael is killed, he also drops a Killer Lance — making the chest redundant. Recruiting Zael and getting the chest yields both Zael AND the lance.
