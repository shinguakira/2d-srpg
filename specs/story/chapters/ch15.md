# Chapter 15: The Burning of Suza

*Arc 3 climax. Not yet implemented.*

## Summary

The southern Sacred Flame goes out. A running fight through a burning shrine-town
against a Kurogane force that is itself fighting a rearguard action against the
blight — and losing.

## Intent

- **Objective**: rout, under a turn limit set by an advancing blight edge
- **The turn of the arc.** For the first time the player watches a Kurogane
  formation break and run from something that is not them. Both armies are on the
  same map fighting different enemies, and the AI should make that legible.
- **The cost**: two flames left. The seal is measurably weaker after this chapter and
  the player should feel the campaign clock speed up.
- **Payoff**: the party proves that Hallowed Ground is not a figure of speech — the
  blight will not cross the shrine precinct, which is the only reason anyone gets out.
