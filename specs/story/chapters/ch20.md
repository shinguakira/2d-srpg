# Chapter 20: The Ashen Wood

*Arc 4 climax. Not yet implemented.*

## Summary

The forest at the foot of the northern mountain — four hundred years dead and still
standing. The blight's oldest ground, and the densest concentration of revenants in
the game.

## Intent

- **Objective**: survive the crossing
- **Escalation**: multiple Colossi, and revenants in armour four centuries out of
  date.
- **The gut punch**: those are the 347. The knights who sealed it are still down
  there, and they are still standing up. The oath was not a clean sacrifice, and the
  songs everyone has been quoting since Ch2 have been lying by omission.
- **Consequence**: this is the chapter that makes Takeshi's position defensible, and
  it lands one arc before the player has to answer it.
