# Chapter 19: The Flame on Shiratake

*Not yet implemented.*

## Summary

The twin-peaked holy mountain above Aso Bay, the northern Sacred Flame still
burning, and the last keeper alive to tend it. Both armies arrive at once.

## Intent

- **Objective**: protect the shrine keeper (`shrineKeeperSaved` — an ending flag)
- **Terrain**: the primeval forest on the mountain's shoulders — heavy cover, brutal
  movement cost, and a summit approach only one unit wide.
- **Lore payoff**: the keeper explains what the 347 actually did, and what relighting
  a shrine costs. The Flamebrand holds 347 embers; relighting Are spends every one of
  them and the sword goes dark for good.
- **Structural point**: Shigeru learns the price of the best ending *here*, with six
  chapters left to carry it, rather than being handed it as a twist at the end.
- **Hina's homecoming**: this is her shrine. She has not been back since Ch1.
