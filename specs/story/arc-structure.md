# Arc Structure — 25 Chapters

The 25-chapter campaign is divided into **5 arcs of 5 chapters each**. Each arc has its own dramatic curve, tone, and thematic focus while building toward the final confrontation with the System.

The meta-narrative core is preserved: Ren remembers 347 cycles. The System is a tragic save manager. Permadeath is real. Awareness spreads as the world breaks down.

---

## Arc Overview

| Arc | Chapters | Title | Theme | Tone | Awareness Spread |
|-----|----------|-------|-------|------|-----------------|
| 1 | 1-5 | **"The Script"** | The familiar loop — everything goes as planned, until it doesn't | Comedy with cracks | 3% → 10% |
| 2 | 6-10 | **"Fractures"** | The system fights back, allies split, Kael dies | Darkening comedy | 10% → 25% |
| 3 | 11-15 | **"Corruption"** | CRP spreads to inhabited areas, moral choices about who to save | Serious with gallows humor | 25% → 50% |
| 4 | 16-20 | **"Awakening"** | Understanding the System's motivation, empathy for the villain | Bittersweet | 50% → 80% |
| 5 | 21-25 | **"The Last Save File"** | Ending the loop, accepting imperfection | Earned catharsis | 80% → 100% |

## Arc 1: "The Script" (Chapters 1-5)

The world runs as expected. Ren speedruns, allies experience things for the first time. Comedy dominates. Small cracks appear by Ch5.

### Ch1 — "Not This Again"
Tutorial chapter. Ren knows every move. Garrek the tutorial boss reveals awareness.
- **Objective**: Rout + Seize
- **Recruits**: Bram (mid-chapter), Lira (mid-chapter)
- **Boss**: Garrek (Fighter, Tier 1 awareness — remembers since cycle 203)
- **Roster after**: Ren, Kael, Senna, Bram, Lira (5 units)

### Ch2 — "The Defector"
Border conflict. Voss defects from the enemy army mid-battle after recognizing the futility of his loop.
- **Objective**: Defeat Boss
- **Recruits**: Voss (mid-chapter, enemy defection)
- **Boss**: Commander Thane (Cavalier, Tier 4 unaware — plays it completely straight)
- **Key event**: Senna discovers the RNG seed is fixed, begins studying it
- **Roster after**: 6 units

### Ch3 — "Scout's Honor"
First chapter with full 6-unit party. Introduces terrain variety, side objectives.
- **Objective**: Rout + visit 2 villages for rewards
- **Recruits**: Nira (Archer, mid-chapter — saved from bandits)
- **Boss**: Bandit Chief (Brigand, Tier 4 unaware)
- **Key event**: First glitched tiles appear on the map. Senna notices.
- **Roster after**: 7 units

### Ch4 — "The Pickpocket"
Multi-objective map. Introduces thief enemies that target villages. A playable thief joins.
- **Objective**: Protect 3 villages + Defeat Boss
- **Recruits**: Coda (Thief, joins if you catch them stealing from a village)
- **Boss**: Merchant Lord (Armor Knight, Tier 4 unaware — just greedy)
- **Key event**: Coda mentions "weird flickering in the market square" — casual CRP exposure
- **Roster after**: 8 units

### Ch5 — "Above the Clouds"
Arc climax. First major glitch event. A pegasus knight from a corrupted region joins.
- **Objective**: Seize (enemy fortress)
- **Recruits**: Yuel (Pegasus Knight, start of chapter — arrives with a warning)
- **Boss**: Sky Marshal (Wyvern Rider, Tier 3 genre-displaced — thinks she's in a flight sim)
- **Key event**: The fortress map glitches mid-battle. Terrain shifts. First Data Void appears. Senna's seed analysis breaks — the System changed it.
- **Arc 1 turning point**: The rules can change. The script is no longer safe.
- **Roster after**: 9 units

## Arc 2: "Fractures" (Chapters 6-10)

The system fights back. New factions emerge. Kael dies at the arc's end. The tone darkens but humor sharpens.

### Ch6 — "New Alliances"
Major recruitment chapter. Multiple units from new factions join as the threat escalates.
- **Objective**: Defeat Boss + Escape (reach exit with all units)
- **Recruits**: Rook (Mercenary, start), Faye (Troubadour, mid-chapter)
- **Boss**: Inquisitor Vael (Dark Mage, Tier 2 partial awareness — detects "anomalies in the data")
- **Key event**: First encounter with organized dark mages. CRP becomes a real threat.
- **Roster after**: 11 units

### Ch7 — "The Seed Breaks"
Senna's crisis chapter. The RNG seed she decoded SHATTERS mid-battle. Outcomes become unpredictable.
- **Objective**: Survive 12 turns (enemies flood in from corrupted spawns)
- **Recruits**: None (pressure chapter)
- **Boss**: None (survival map, no boss)
- **Key event**: Senna's combat forecasts become unreliable. Hit rates flicker. The System is actively destabilizing combat math.
- **Roster after**: 11 units

### Ch8 — "The Last Ride" ★ KAEL'S DEATH ★
Kael's final chapter. He goes out protecting the party. His death is scripted but should feel like it could have been prevented.
- **Objective**: Seize (fortress throne)
- **Recruits**: None
- **Boss**: General Morryn (Soldier, Tier 2 partial awareness — begging for mercy, knows he's killed Kael before)
- **Key event**: Kael holds the rear guard. Story event at a specific turn removes him from player control. He fights alone against reinforcements and falls. The player watches.
- **Mechanical impact**: Lose 7 MOV cavalry, dual weapons (sword+lance), Canto, and the emotional anchor. Grief trauma skill hits all party members (-3 all stats for 2 chapters).
- **Roster after**: 10 units (Kael permanently gone)

### Ch9 — "The Void Left Behind"
Aftermath chapter. The party operates with a hole. New unit must fill Kael's role.
- **Objective**: Rout (smaller map, fewer enemies — the party is weakened)
- **Recruits**: Orin (Dancer, mid-chapter — appears to "cheer up" the party. Genre: thinks this is a musical)
- **Boss**: Raider Captain (Cavalier, Tier 4 unaware)
- **Key event**: First chapter without Kael. Every formation the player built around him is broken. Orin's Dance ability (refresh ally action) partially compensates for lost action economy.
- **Roster after**: 11 units

### Ch10 — "What We Carry"
Arc 2 climax. First confrontation with a System-level threat. The party must choose what to protect.
- **Objective**: Protect NPC + Defeat Boss (dual objective)
- **Recruits**: None
- **Boss**: Void Herald (Corrupted, Tier 5 System-level — the System's first direct agent)
- **Key event**: The System creates a construct soldier and sends it. Not ???_CORRUPTED yet — a prototype. Party barely survives. Senna begins theorizing how to fight the System directly.
- **Arc 2 turning point**: The System is no longer passive. It will kill them if it can.
- **Roster after**: 11 units

## Arc 3: "Corruption" (Chapters 11-15)

Corruption spreads to populated areas. Moral choices about who to save. The darkest arc. First promotions available.

### Ch11 — "Spreading Plague"
Corruption reaches inhabited areas. Protect civilians while fighting corrupted enemies.
- **Objective**: Protect 5 NPC villagers (they flee toward exits, enemies chase them)
- **Recruits**: Kira (Shaman/Dark Mage defector, mid-chapter — was spreading corruption, switches sides)
- **Boss**: Corrupted Priest (Dark Mage, Tier 3 genre-displaced — thinks this is a horror game)
- **Key event**: First chapter where CRP is a constant, unavoidable pressure. Glitched terrain everywhere.
- **Roster after**: 12 units

### Ch12 — "The Turning" ★ CORRUPTION LOSS ★
A party member turns. One of the newer recruits hits CRP 100 and becomes an enemy mid-battle.
- **Objective**: Defeat Boss (the turned ally IS the boss)
- **Recruits**: None
- **Boss**: [Corrupted Ally] (varies based on who was most exposed to CRP — likely the one with highest CRP)
- **Key event**: A party member the player invested in turns hostile. Must be defeated. Cannot be saved. Permadeath via corruption.
- **Mechanical impact**: Lose a leveled unit. Party morale shattered. First time the player realizes CRP management matters.
- **Roster after**: 11 units

### Ch13 — "Second Chances"
A partially corrupted recruit joins — can they be saved? Introduces the CRP management metagame.
- **Objective**: Seize + keep new recruit alive
- **Recruits**: Zael (Wyvern Rider, starts as enemy, recruitable mid-chapter — CRP at 40, ticking up)
- **Boss**: Warden (General, Tier 4 unaware — just follows orders)
- **Key event**: Zael is corrupted but fighting it. If the player uses Purify staves and manages CRP, they can be kept. If not, they turn in a later chapter. A ticking bomb in the roster.
- **Roster after**: 12 units

### Ch14 — "The Monastery"
A place of peace — a monastery where a monk fights corruption. Quieter chapter, character-focused.
- **Objective**: Defeat Boss (dark mage siege on the monastery)
- **Recruits**: Elara (Monk/Light Mage, start — the monastery's protector)
- **Boss**: Shadow Archon (Dark Mage, Tier 2 partial awareness — debates theology with Elara)
- **Key event**: Light magic introduced as anti-corruption tool. Elara's light tomes reduce CRP on hit. First reliable way to fight corruption offensively.
- **Promotion items**: First Master Seal available (monastery reward)
- **Roster after**: 13 units

### Ch15 — "The Rally"
Arc 3 climax. The party rallies after the corruption crisis. Big battle, big speeches, renewed purpose.
- **Objective**: Rout (large map, 20+ enemies, multiple approach routes)
- **Recruits**: None (everyone rallies)
- **Boss**: Corruption Core (Corrupted, Tier 5 — a concentrated mass of corrupted data, not humanoid)
- **Key event**: Senna discovers a "patch" — a way to stabilize local terrain using LOOP data. Ren spends LOOP to stabilize the map. First major LOOP expenditure.
- **Arc 3 turning point**: Corruption can be fought. The party has tools now. But at what cost to Ren's LOOP?
- **Roster after**: 13 units (or 12 if Zael turned)

## Arc 4: "Awakening" (Chapters 16-20)

Understanding why the System does what it does. Empathy for the villain. Comedy returns warmer. Master class promotions begin appearing.

### Ch16 — "Through Its Eyes"
The System's perspective. A unique chapter where narration comes from the System itself.
- **Objective**: Survive (but the System keeps spawning weaker enemies — it's testing, not killing)
- **Recruits**: None
- **Boss**: None (the chapter IS the System talking)
- **Key event**: The System explains itself. It has been trying to find a "perfect playthrough" where nobody dies. 347 cycles. It resets every time someone dies because it can't accept the loss. It's not evil — it's grieving.
- **Roster after**: 13 units

### Ch17 — "Data Recovery"
Retrieve corrupted data that reveals the System's full history. Dungeon-style map with fog of war.
- **Objective**: Capture 3 data nodes (specific tiles) + Escape
- **Recruits**: None
- **Boss**: Memory Guardian (Corrupted, Tier 5 — an old backup of the System's defense protocol)
- **Key event**: The data reveals: Cycle #1 was a real game. A real player. The System was the save manager. The player stopped playing. The System kept going, trying to reach the ending for a player who would never return.
- **Roster after**: 13 units

### Ch18 — "Legacy Weapons"
Legendary weapons begin appearing — artifacts from past cycles, encoded with memory data.
- **Objective**: Defeat Boss + collect 2 legendary weapons from treasure tiles
- **Recruits**: Ghael (Armor Knight, mid-chapter — a former boss from a past arc who switched sides after witnessing the System's truth)
- **Boss**: Relic Warden (General, Tier 3 genre-displaced — thinks this is a dungeon crawler, guards loot)
- **Key event**: First legendary weapons acquired. These are Prf weapons tied to the story — pieces of cycle data forged into weapon form.
- **Master class items**: First Master class seal available
- **Roster after**: 14 units

### Ch19 — "The Offer"
The System offers a deal: one more reset. It will fix everything. No one has to die.
- **Objective**: Reach the System's terminal (seize) — but reaching it triggers a choice event
- **Recruits**: None
- **Boss**: System Emissary (System Construct, Tier 5 — a perfect replica of Ren from cycle #1)
- **Key event**: Choice: accept the reset or refuse. This is not a real choice — the game continues either way. But the dialogue changes. Ren refuses. "Even if the ending is imperfect, it's OURS."
- **LOY impact**: Major LOY shifts based on party members' reactions to Ren's refusal
- **Roster after**: 14 units

### Ch20 — "Point of No Return"
Arc 4 climax. The System withdraws its cooperation. The world begins unraveling.
- **Objective**: Seize (the System's gateway to the final region)
- **Recruits**: Echo (System Construct with free will, Tier 5 — the System created them but they chose to leave)
- **Boss**: System Warden (System Construct, Tier 5 — the System's last guardian before the final region)
- **Key event**: Ren commits. No more resets. Whatever happens in the next 5 chapters is final. The System begins deploying everything.
- **Arc 4 turning point**: The enemy is no longer the System's cruelty — it's the System's love. It resets because it doesn't want anyone to die. Ren is choosing death (for some) over eternity (for all).
- **Roster after**: 15 units

## Arc 5: "The Last Save File" (Chapters 21-25)

Ending the loop. The hardest chapters. Comedy, tears, and resolution. The game ends.

### Ch21 — "The Long March"
First chapter in the System's core territory. Maps are unstable. Terrain shifts per turn.
- **Objective**: Reach exit (advance through shifting terrain)
- **Recruits**: None (final roster locked)
- **Boss**: Corruption Titan (Corrupted, Tier 5 — a massive data mass blocking the path)
- **Key event**: Continuous reinforcements from Data Voids. The party must push through, not fight every enemy. Time pressure via spreading corruption.
- **Roster after**: 15 units (deployment limit: 12)

### Ch22 — "Ghosts of Cycles Past"
Nostalgic chapter. Echoes of past cycles manifest as phantom enemies — they look like allies from cycle #1.
- **Objective**: Defeat Boss + survive phantom waves
- **Recruits**: None
- **Boss**: Phantom Commander (System echo of cycle #1's version of Ren — a version who never got tired)
- **Key event**: Callbacks to earlier chapters. Garrek's voice echoes. Phantom Kael appears but isn't hostile — he salutes and vanishes. Character moments. The party says things they've been holding back.

### Ch23 — "The Penultimate"
The second-hardest chapter. The System throws everything at once. All enemy types, maximum difficulty.
- **Objective**: Rout (every enemy type in the game appears on one map)
- **Recruits**: None
- **Boss**: System General (System Construct, Tier 5 — perfect stats, perfect AI, the System's best)
- **Key event**: If any party member has CRP > 60, the System tries to turn them mid-battle. Final CRP check. Elara/Kira's anti-corruption abilities are critical.
- **Mechanical note**: Hardest combat chapter. Full deployment (12 units). Every tool the player has learned is needed.

### Ch24 — "???_CORRUPTED" ★ THE KAEL REVELATION ★
The System's last love letter. The corrupted boss is revealed: it's reconstructed Kael data.
- **Objective**: Defeat ???_CORRUPTED (3-phase boss fight)
- **Recruits**: None
- **Boss**: ???_CORRUPTED (Kael's reconstructed data, cycling weapon type, impossible stats)
  - Phase 1: ???_CORRUPTED fights with Kael's moveset. The party recognizes him.
  - Phase 2: Senna discovers the exploit — weapon triangle still works, weapon type cycles predictably.
  - Phase 3: Hit with weapon advantage 3 times to peel corruption layers. Kael's voice emerges briefly.
- **Key event**: The System didn't corrupt Kael to weaponize him. It tried to SAVE him. It reconstructed his data from memory. But the data was too damaged. It's a mercy kill.
- **Emotional core**: "He's not Kael. But he remembers being Kael."

### Ch25 — "The Last Save File"
The final chapter. Ren seizes the last throne. The loop ends.
- **Objective**: Seize (the System's core — the final throne)
- **Recruits**: None
- **Boss**: The System (not a unit — the MAP itself. Terrain shifts to block progress. Tiles heal enemies. The map IS the boss.)
  - Victory condition: Ren reaches the center tile and Seizes. But the path keeps changing.
  - Senna navigates. Bram breaks walls. Lira heals through corruption. Voss holds chokepoints. Everyone has a role.
- **Final dialogue**: The System speaks. Not as an enemy. As a save manager that ran 347 cycles trying to find a playthrough where everyone lives.
  - **[SYSTEM]**: "I just wanted... one perfect save."
  - **Ren**: "There is no perfect save. There's just... the one we finish."
  - **[SYSTEM]**: "...Save complete. Thank you for playing."
- **Ending**: No reset. Cycle #347 ends. The game file closes. Credits roll over a still image of the party — imperfect, grieving, alive.

## Open Questions

- **Kael's data in Ch24**: Should the player be able to "save" Kael's data (recruit a weakened version) or is the mercy kill mandatory?
- **Echo's class**: System Construct is unique — what weapon type? What stats? Fully custom or reskinned existing class?
- **Chapter length scaling**: Should later chapters be longer (more turns, bigger maps) or keep consistent size?
