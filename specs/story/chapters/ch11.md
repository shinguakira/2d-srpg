# Chapter 11: "Spreading Plague"

## Summary

The first chapter of Arc 3. Corruption reaches inhabited areas — a border town is being consumed in real time. Glitched terrain tiles spread across the map each turn, and five NPC villagers flee toward exits while corrupted enemies chase them. The party must protect the civilians while fighting enemies whose stats shift unpredictably. Mid-chapter, Kira — a Shaman who was actively spreading corruption — switches sides when she realizes the corruption is consuming its own agents. The boss is a Corrupted General, a former Imperial officer whose mind has been shattered by the System's data overwrite. This is the chapter where CRP becomes an unavoidable constant threat rather than a background mechanic.

## Prologue Dialogue

**Scene**: A market town at the edge of the corrupted zone. Tiles flicker between normal terrain and glitched static. Civilians stumble through streets that shift under their feet. The sky has a faint grid pattern.

- **Senna**: "The corruption spread faster than I predicted. This town should have been safe for another week."
- **Ren**: "It's accelerating. Every chapter it gets worse."
- **Voss**: "There are civilians still in the market square. Five — no, six — trying to reach the east and south gates."
- **Nira**: "And those things in the corrupted zone are moving toward them. Not toward us. Toward THEM."
- **Ren**: "The corruption targets unprotected data first. Civilians don't have combat stats — they're the easiest to overwrite."
- **Bram**: "Then we get between them and those things. Simple."
- **Senna**: "Nothing about this is simple, Bram. Look at the terrain — it's CHANGING. Movement costs will shift every turn. Pathfinding becomes unreliable."
- **Orin**: "A stage that rearranges itself mid-performance! Experimental theater at its FINEST!"
- **Ren**: "Protect the villagers. Get them to the exits. Kill anything corrupted that gets close. Move."

## Mid-Battle Events

### Turn 2 — Glitched Terrain Spreads

Two tiles near the town center corrupt, shifting from road to glitched terrain (halved movement, no defense bonus, CRP +5 per turn for units standing on them). A corrupted soldier spawns from one.

- **Senna**: "The corrupted tiles are generating enemies. Not reinforcements — the TERRAIN is turning into soldiers."
- **Coda**: "That's not fair. The ground is fighting us too?"
- **Senna**: "CRP warning — anyone standing on glitched tiles takes corruption buildup. Stay off them."
- **Lira**: "I can't heal corruption. Only HP. We need something else."
- **Ren**: "We don't have it yet. Avoid the tiles. Protect the villagers. One problem at a time."

### Turn 4 — Kira Defects

A hooded Shaman on the enemy side stops advancing. She stares at her hands, which crackle with dark energy that's eating into her fingers. She turns toward the party.

- **Kira**: "...It's eating me too."
- **Ren**: "Who are you?"
- **Kira**: "I was spreading it. The corruption. They told me it was purification — burning out the old data to make room for the new. But it doesn't stop. It doesn't STOP."
- **Senna**: "Your CRP is at 60. You're partially corrupted already."
- **Kira**: "I know what I am. I know what I did. Let me help undo it. I have Nosferatu — I can drain HP from the corrupted. It's the only thing that works on them."
- **Ren**: "...Fine. Stay close to Lira. If your CRP hits 80, we pull you out."
- **Kira**: *(quietly)* "Thank you. I don't deserve it."

**[Kira joins. Shaman, Level 16. Nosferatu (HP drain). CRP starts at 60. Tier 2 partial awareness.]**

### Turn 7 — Villager Panic

Two of the five villagers reach exits safely. The remaining three cluster near the south gate, blocked by a corrupted knight. One villager turns back toward the corrupted zone in panic.

- **Nira**: "The villager's running the WRONG WAY! Into the corrupted tiles!"
- **Yuel**: "I can reach her — flying ignores terrain."
- **Ren**: "Go. Yuel intercepts the panicking villager. Everyone else, clear the south gate."
- **Bram**: *(smashing a corrupted soldier)* "These things don't feel pain. I hit it and it just... recalculates."
- **Kira**: "They're not people anymore. They're processes. The person who was inside is gone."
- **Bram**: "...That's worse."

## Boss Fight — The Corrupted General

The Corrupted General. Former Imperial officer, now a shambling data error in armor. Lance, Aggressive (erratic) AI, Tier varies (broken). Level 18, 58 HP. Stats randomize each turn (all stats shift ±3). May heal corruption damage instead of attacking. Unpredictable movement pattern.

- **Pre-combat**:
  - **Corrupted General**: "O̵r̸d̵e̵r̴s̶.̷ ̶I̵ ̵h̵a̶d̴ ̴o̵r̵d̸e̴r̶s̶.̴ ̸W̵h̷a̶t̷ ̸w̸e̸r̶e̵ ̶t̵h̸e̴y̵?̵"
  - **Ren**: "He's still in there. Somewhere."
  - **Kira**: "No. He's not. I've seen what corruption does from the inside. That voice is an echo. The person is already overwritten."
  - **Ren**: "...Then we end it."

**Mechanical note**: The Corrupted General's stat randomization makes him impossible to forecast reliably. The player must commit to attacking on turns when his DEF/SPD roll low, and retreat when they roll high. Kira's Nosferatu is effective — dark magic ignores some of his corruption-based resistance.

- **Post-combat**:
  - **Corrupted General**: *(collapsing, voice clearing for one moment)* "...Tell my wife I held the line."
  - *(Static. He dissolves into corrupted data.)*
  - **Kira**: *(looking away)* "He didn't hold anything. He was a weapon pointed at civilians."
  - **Senna**: "He was a person before the corruption took him. Remember that."

## Epilogue Dialogue

**Scene**: The town, partially saved. Three of five villagers escaped. Two tiles of the market square are permanently corrupted — glitched terrain that won't revert. Kira sits apart from the group.

- **Senna**: "We saved three. Lost two. The corruption consumed them before they reached the gates."
- **Ren**: "Three out of five. That's... not enough."
- **Voss**: "It's more than zero. In my experience, that's the bar now."
- **Kira**: *(apart from the group)* "I did this. Not just here — three other towns. I spread it to THREE other towns before I realized."
- **Lira**: "And now you're fighting it."
- **Kira**: "That doesn't bring back the people in those towns."
- **Lira**: "No. It doesn't. But it changes what happens in the next one."
- **Senna**: "CRP report. Kira is at 62 — stable for now. Bram took corruption from a glitched tile — CRP 15. Everyone else is clean. But this is going to get worse. We need a way to REDUCE corruption, not just avoid it."
- **Ren**: "Senna. The data from Maren's scrolls — anything about purification?"
- **Senna**: "References to light magic. Old monastery texts. There's a place in the highlands — a monastery that was studying corruption before the System accelerated it."
- **Ren**: "Then that's where we go."

## Story Beats Delivered

- CRP becomes a constant, unavoidable threat — glitched terrain spreads and corrupted enemies appear from the map itself
- Kira's recruitment: a guilty defector who was actively spreading corruption, joins mid-chapter with high CRP (60)
- Protect objective introduces civilian AI — NPC villagers flee toward exits, enemies prioritize them
- The Corrupted General's stat randomization teaches players that corruption makes combat unpredictable
- Nosferatu introduced as effective anti-corruption tool (dark magic draining corrupted units)
- The "person inside is gone" theme established — corruption is not mind control, it's overwriting
- Kira's guilt and Lira's pragmatic compassion set up their dynamic for the arc
- Senna identifies the monastery as the next destination, threading into Ch14
- Tone shift: Arc 3 is darker. Three civilians saved out of five is treated as a partial failure, not a victory
