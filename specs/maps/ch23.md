# Chapter 23 Map: "The Penultimate"

## Overview
- Grid size: 22×22 total (two 11×22 sections separated by partition wall)
- Tileset: Circuit, wall (partition), void, plain, mountain, forest, fort, road
- Terrain focus: Split map. Left section (cols 0-10) has heavy terrain — mountains, forests, chokepoints. Right section (cols 11-21) is open ground with minimal cover. A data partition wall at col 10-11 separates the halves until both Sentinels drop below 30% HP.

## Layout
A massive chamber bisected by a shimmering data partition wall running north-south at cols 10-11 (2-tile-wide wall, impassable). **Left section** (cols 0-10): Mountainous terrain. Rows 18-21 are the left deployment zone — road and plain tiles. Mountains at (10, 2-4), (14, 6-8), and (6, 1-3) create chokepoints. Forests at (12, 3-5) and (8, 5-7) provide cover. Forts at (10, 5) and (6, 8). Sentinel_L sits at (2, 5). **Right section** (cols 11-21): Open field. Rows 18-21 are the right deployment zone. Flat circuit and plain tiles with minimal walls. A single fort at (10, 16). Long sightlines favor ranged combat. Sentinel_R holds at (2, 16). When both Sentinels drop below 30% HP, the partition wall at cols 10-11 shatters, merging both halves. Snow weather: +1 movement cost, -2 SPD all units.

## Tactical Notes
- **Team composition**: Left section has mountains and forests — send physical fighters and tanks (Shigeru, Goro, Genzo, Ghael). Right section is open ground with high-DEF enemies — send mages and ranged units (Kanna, Sayo, Kira, Echo). Each side needs at least one healer.
- **Synchronization**: Both Sentinels must be defeated within 3 turns of each other. If one dies too early, the survivor absorbs +10 all stats. Chip both down evenly, then burst them in the same turn window. Communication (the partition weakens as HP drops) helps gauge timing.
- **CRP loyalty check**: Turn 5's CRP check targets the highest-CRP unit. If that unit has CRP < 60, no effect. If CRP ≥ 60, they're uncontrollable for 3 turns. Keep high-CRP units near healers to reduce confusion duration. Managing CRP throughout the campaign pays off here.
- **Snow weather**: +1 movement cost and -2 SPD for all units. This slows everyone — cavalry and fliers feel it most. Plan extra turns for positioning.
- **Partition mechanics**: The wall thins as Sentinels take damage. At 30% HP on both, it shatters. Once merged, converge on whichever Sentinel has more HP remaining. The reunited army is far stronger than either half alone.
