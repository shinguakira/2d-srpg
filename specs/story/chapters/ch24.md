# Chapter 24: "???_CORRUPTED"

## Summary

The emotional climax of the entire game. The System's innermost chamber holds its last creation: a corrupted unit built from reconstructed save data — Kael's data. The System didn't weaponize Kael. It tried to SAVE him. Reconstructed him from fragments of 347 cycles of memory, growth rates, stats, combat logs. But the data was too damaged. What remains is ???_CORRUPTED — a glitching, cycling weapon-type boss that fights with Kael's moveset but can't remember who it is. The three-phase fight peels away corruption layers until the unit underneath is revealed. Senna discovers the exploit. Ren faces what's left of his friend. And the party has to let go — for real, for the last time. This is the chapter that earns the ending.

**Scene**: The System's innermost chamber. It's quiet. No enemies. No shifting terrain. Just a single figure standing in the center of the room, flickering, its sprite cycling between forms. The party enters and stops.

- **Senna**: *(scanning)* "A reconstructed unit. The System built it from... from save data. Hundreds of fragments stitched together. Stats, growth rates, combat logs, position data—"

- `[SYSTEM: ...TO BRING SOMEONE BACK.]`

## Mid-Battle Events

### Turn 1 — Phase 1: The Unknown

???_CORRUPTED activates. Its sprite is unreadable — a mass of glitching pixel data. It fights with a moveset that feels familiar but wrong. Its weapon cycles each turn: sword, lance, axe, fire, thunder, wind.

- **???_CORRUPTED**: `"D̷a̸t̶a̷ ̵l̶o̵a̸d̷i̵n̶g̶.̷.̸.̷ ̷u̸n̵i̴t̵ ̸n̸a̸m̸e̵:̶ ̵?̴?̵?̵.̷ ̴C̶l̶a̷s̷s̸:̵ ̷?̴?̵?̷.̴ ̴L̵o̵r̸d̴:̶ ̸?̸?̶?̶.̵ ̸E̷N̴G̵A̴G̸E̸.̶"`
- **Nira**: "It moves like... I've seen that footwork before."
- **Bram**: "That attack pattern. The way it positions. That's—"
- **Ren**: "I SAID don't say it."

**Mechanical note**: Phase 1 HP shows as 999. Normal attacks deal damage but HP resets to 999 each turn. The boss cycles weapons on a fixed rotation: sword → lance → axe → fire → thunder → wind. Its AI is Erratic — targets random units, moves unpredictably. It uses Kael's combat animations.

### Turn 3 — Recognition

The party can no longer deny it. The corrupted unit's movement pattern, its positioning instincts, its stat spread underneath the corruption — it's all compiled from one source.

- **Lira**: "That's Kael's stance. When he guards. That exact angle — I healed him a hundred times, I KNOW that stance."
- **Bram**: *(voice breaking)* "The System... it took his data and tried to..."
- **Senna**: "It wasn't trying to weaponize him. Look at the reconstruction logs — there are 347 attempts. Every cycle, the System tried to rebuild him. Every cycle, the data degraded further."
- **Echo**: `[THE SYSTEM... LOVED KAEL_UNIT. THE WAY THE SYSTEM LOVES ALL UNITS. IT COULD NOT ACCEPT THE LOSS.]`
- `[SYSTEM: ...KAEL_UNIT WAS BRAVE. THIS SYSTEM DOES NOT UNDERSTAND BRAVERY. BUT THIS SYSTEM UNDERSTANDS LOSS. AND THIS SYSTEM TRIED TO FIX IT.]`
- **Ren**: *(quietly)* "You couldn't fix it. Some things can't be compiled back together."

### Turn 5 — Phase 2: Senna's Exploit

Senna has been watching the weapon cycling. She sees the pattern. The breakthrough moment.

- **Senna**: "WAIT. I see it. The weapon triangle code — it's ORIGINAL. It wasn't overwritten by the corruption."
- **Ren**: "What does that mean?"
- **Senna**: "The corruption rewrote its stats, its HP, its AI — but the weapon triangle interaction is hardcoded. It can't corrupt the RULES. If we hit it with advantage on the exact turn its weapon cycles to the vulnerable type—"
- **Ren**: "We can crash its data."
- **Senna**: "Not crash. CORRECT. Replace the corrupted values with real ones. Make it a real unit again."
- **Lira**: "You want to HEAL the final boss?"
- **Senna**: "I want to fix a bug."

**Mechanical note**: Phase 2 begins. The player must attack ???_CORRUPTED with weapon triangle advantage matching its current cycling weapon. Sword turn → attack with lance. Lance turn → attack with sword. Axe turn → hit with sword. Fire turn → use wind magic. Each correct hit strips one corruption layer. HP drops: 999 → 60 → 40 → real stats. Three successful hits needed. Incorrect attacks still deal normal damage but don't strip layers.

### Turn 7 — Phase 3: Peeling the Corruption

The first corruption layer strips away. The sprite stabilizes slightly — less glitching, recognizable fragments of a lance knight showing through.

- **???_CORRUPTED**: `"...cy...cle... 34...8? This... was not... scheduled..."`
- **Ren**: "Keep going! Two more layers!"

Second layer stripped. The sprite is mostly clear now. The party can see him — damaged, incomplete, but HIM.

- **???_CORRUPTED**: `"...lance... I had a lance. I stood... in front of... someone important. I stood in front of—"`
- **Bram**: "REN! He stood in front of Ren! EVERYONE KNOWS THAT!"

Third and final hit. The corruption shatters. The unit's data resolves. HP: 1. Stats: Kael's original stats from Chapter 1. The sprite is clear — it's Kael. Damaged, flickering at the edges, but Kael.

## Boss Fight — ???_CORRUPTED

???_CORRUPTED. Glitched unit (weapon cycles each turn: sword → lance → axe → fire → thunder → wind), Erratic AI, Tier 5. Level 33. HP 999 → 60 → 40 → Kael's real stats (effectively 1 HP when fully decorrrupted).

**Phase 1 (HP 999)**: Erratic movement, random targeting, Kael's animations. Normal damage resets each turn. Weapon cycling is the only vulnerability.

**Phase 2 (HP 60, after first correction)**: More stable. AI shifts from Erratic to Aggressive — charges the nearest unit. Still cycling weapons but slower. The sprite begins to resolve.

**Phase 3 (HP 40, after second correction)**: Nearly stable. AI shifts to Guard — holds position. Weapon cycling slows further. The player can see Kael's form. Third correction hit resolves the unit completely.

- **Post-resolution** *(Kael's data fades)*:
  - `[SYSTEM: ...KAEL_UNIT DATA — IRRECOVERABLE. RECONSTRUCTION FAILED. THIS SYSTEM... APOLOGIZES.]`
  - **Ren**: "You didn't fail. You tried to save him. That's... more than I expected from you."
  - `[SYSTEM: THIS SYSTEM DOES NOT UNDERSTAND WHY UNITS CHOOSE TO END. BUT THIS SYSTEM UNDERSTANDS THAT KAEL_UNIT CHOSE. ...THIS SYSTEM WILL STOP TRYING TO UNDO THAT CHOICE.]`
  - **Lira**: *(quietly)* "Goodbye, Kael. For the last time."
  - **Bram**: *(punches the wall, then leans against it)* "...Stupid. Brave. STUPID."

**[???_CORRUPTED resolved. The path to the System's core opens. Chapter complete.]**
