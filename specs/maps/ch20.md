# Chapter 20 Map: "Point of No Return"

## Overview
- Grid size: 22×24
- Tileset: Circuit, void, plain (fractured ground), fort, wall, data_node, bridge, throne (gateway)
- Terrain focus: Fractured landscape with a massive data gateway. Terrain corrupts mid-battle, shrinking the playable area. The System's last conventional defense.

## Layout
A sprawling fractured battlefield. The south (rows 20-23) is the staging area — stable plain tiles with two forts at (21, 7) and (21, 16). The mid-field (rows 10-19) is open fractured ground dotted with circuit tiles and scattered wall segments. Void patches already exist at the corners: (10, 0-2), (10, 21-23), (19, 0-1), (19, 22-23). A bridge at (14, 11-12) crosses a void chasm running east-west at row 14 (cols 0-10 and 13-23 are void). The north half (rows 4-13) is the gateway approach — narrowing terrain as walls funnel toward the gateway throne at (1, 11). Echo's Core tile sits at (4, 14), 3 spaces from the gateway. Fort tiles at (7, 8) and (7, 14) provide defensive positions on the approach. The gateway is flanked by construct walls. Terrain corruption starts Turn 2: random tiles become void every 2 turns, compressing the battlefield toward the center.

## Tactical Notes
- **Bridge bottleneck**: The void chasm at row 14 forces all ground units through a 2-tile bridge at (14, 11-12). Fliers (Yuel, Zael if recruited) bypass this entirely. Use tanks to hold the bridge while ranged units cross.
- **Terrain corruption**: Every 2 turns, 2-3 tiles become void. The corruption avoids the direct path to the gateway — the System wants the party to arrive, just weakened. Plan movement along the center; edges corrupt first.
- **Echo's Core choice**: Echo's Core tile at (4, 14) must be reached BEFORE the gateway is seized. If Echo reaches it, Echo is permanently stabilized. If not, Echo loses 5 HP/turn in all future chapters. This is a meaningful resource commitment — Echo must push through the most dangerous part of the map.
- **Sentinel Omega phases**: Phase 1 (physical, high DEF) demands magic. Phase 2 (magic, high RES, more mobile) demands physical burst. The phase swap at 40 HP forces army versatility. Save magic users for Phase 1, then swap to physical attackers for Phase 2.
- **Shrinking battlefield**: By Turn 10, the playable area is significantly smaller. Don't dawdle in the staging area — push north immediately. Units left behind may find their terrain corrupted underneath them.
- **Echo recruitment**: Echo joins as a green unit at Turn 5. They can equip any weapon type — assign based on what the party lacks. Echo's flat 30% growths make them a jack-of-all-trades.
- **Gateway guards**: The two Escort guards at (3, 9) and (3, 13) reposition to block. Bait one out with a ranged attack, then rush the gap. Orin's Dance enables a tank to move in and a damage dealer to follow in the same turn.
