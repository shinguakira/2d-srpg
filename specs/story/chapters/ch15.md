# Chapter 15: Scorched Sands

*Arc 3 climax. Not yet implemented.*

## Summary

A running battle across open sand against a Kurogane army that is fighting a
rearguard action against the blight at the same time — and losing.

## Intent

- **Objective**: rout, under a turn limit set by an advancing blight edge
- **The turn of the arc.** For the first time the player watches a Kurogane
  formation break and run from something that is not them. Enemy and player units
  are on the same map fighting different enemies, and the AI should make that legible.
- **Arc close**: the desert alliance is real, the second Sacred Flame is named, and
  the party proves that Hallowed Ground is not a figure of speech — the blight will
  not cross it.
