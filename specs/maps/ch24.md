## Layout
A circular arena within a square grid. The outer ring (rows 0-2, 17-19, cols 0-2, 17-19) is void tiles — impassable and damaging. The corruption ring (rows 3, 16, col 3, col 16) is circuit tiles with no defensive bonus. The inner arena (rows 4-15, cols 4-15) is flat plain tiles — the entire fight happens here. Four stone pillars (wall tiles) at (6, 6), (6, 13), (13, 6), (13, 13) provide the only cover (1-tile walls, can block ranged attacks from one direction). ???_CORRUPTED starts at the exact center: (10, 10). Four data_node tiles at (5, 10), (14, 10), (10, 5), (10, 14) mark the cardinal positions — stepping on them during the correct weapon cycle turn deals bonus damage to the boss. No forts. No terrain advantage. The arena is deliberately bare. Player deploys along the south inner edge (rows 14-15).

## Tactical Notes
- **Weapon cycle tracking**: The boss cycles weapons each turn: sword → lance → axe → fire → thunder → wind. To strip corruption, attack with the weapon that has ADVANTAGE on the current cycle turn. Sword turn → hit with lance. Lance turn → hit with sword. Axe turn → hit with sword. Fire turn → hit with wind magic. Thunder turn → hit with fire magic. Wind turn → hit with thunder magic.
- **Phase 1 patience**: HP 999 resets each turn. Normal damage is meaningless until Kanna discovers the exploit on Turn 5. Use Phase 1 to position units around the arena and heal. The boss's erratic AI means no unit is targeted consistently.
- **Kagura is critical**: Dance enables two weapon-triangle hits in one turn, potentially stripping two corruption layers in a single round. Position Kagura adjacent to your best weapon-triangle attacker.
- **Data node bonus**: Standing on a cardinal data_node tile when delivering a weapon-triangle hit adds +5 bonus damage. Not required but speeds up the fight significantly.
- **Pillar cover**: The four pillars are the only cover. Use them to block the boss's ranged attacks during magic weapon turns (fire/thunder/wind). Position healers behind pillars.
- **Emotional, not numerical**: This fight has no reinforcements, no terrain tricks, no time pressure. The difficulty is mechanical precision and the emotional weight of fighting Akira's data. The arena's emptiness is deliberate — nothing to hide behind, nothing to distract from the truth.
- **Incorrect hits**: Attacking with the wrong weapon type still deals normal damage but does NOT strip corruption layers. The boss's HP resets each turn in Phase 1 anyway, so incorrect hits are wasted actions.
- **Unit positioning**: Spread units around the arena perimeter so that someone with the correct weapon type is always within striking range, regardless of which direction the boss moves in its erratic pattern.
- **Master Crown drop**: ???_CORRUPTED drops the final Master Crown on resolution. This is the last promotion item in the game — save it for whoever benefits most in Ch25.
