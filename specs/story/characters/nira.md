# Nira — Archer (The Sharpshooter)

- **Class**: Archer (bow)
- **Awareness Tier**: 4 (Unaware)

## Personality

Quiet, precise, methodical. Speaks in short sentences — sometimes just one word. Prefers action over words. If she can answer with a nod, she will. Not cold, just efficient. Wastes nothing: not arrows, not breath, not time.

## Meta-Angle

The most "normal" person in the party. While everyone else debates metaphysics and reality loops, Nira shoots things. She has zero interest in awareness, cycles, or the nature of existence. She is a grounding force of pure pragmatism.

- "Why does it matter? The target's still there."
- Treats Ren's cycle knowledge as useful battlefield intel, nothing more — doesn't question WHY he knows it
- When others panic about glitches, Nira checks her bowstring
- Her lack of curiosity is actually a strength — corruption has nothing to latch onto

## What She Learns / What She Doesn't Know

- Never learns the full truth about cycles or the System — doesn't need to
- Slowly realizes her "normal" perspective is what holds the party together when others spiral
- Learns to use words when silence isn't enough — specifically to talk Yuel down from panic
- Doesn't understand why Senna keeps staring at her like she's a research subject

## Relationships

- **Bram**: Tolerates his noise. He's loud, she's quiet — somehow it works. She appreciates that he never asks her to explain herself. He appreciates that she never tells him to shut up.
- **Senna**: Respects Senna's precision. Two methodical minds, different domains. Senna measures data; Nira measures distance. Their supports are comfortable silences broken by tactical observations.
- **Ren**: Treats his future-knowledge as battlefield intel. Doesn't ask how he knows, just adjusts her aim.
- **Yuel**: Becomes Yuel's anchor. When Yuel spirals about the sky glitching, Nira points at a target and says "Focus."

## Arc

1. **Arc 1 (Ch3-5)**: Rescued from bandits. Quiet gratitude. Immediately useful — her bow covers the party's 2-range gap. Says almost nothing but her actions speak.
2. **Arc 2 (Ch6-10)**: Becomes the party's emotional anchor by NOT reacting to the chaos. When Kael dies and everyone breaks down, Nira picks up her bow and says "What's the next target?" It sounds heartless but it's what everyone needs to hear.
3. **Arc 3 (Ch11-15)**: Corruption threatens the party. Nira's total lack of meta-awareness makes her nearly immune. She becomes the reliable constant.
4. **Arc 4-5 (Ch16-25)**: Finally uses words when it matters. Her speech before the final battle is three sentences long and it's the most anyone's ever heard her say at once.

## Key Lines

- *"Breathe in. Aim. Release. Everything else is noise."*
- *"I don't need to understand the world to protect the people in it."*
- *"You talk too much."* (to Bram — said with the faintest smile)
- *"The sky's wrong? Fine. My arrows still fly straight."*
