# Promotion System

Three-tier class progression: **Base → Promoted → Master**. Promotion unlocks higher stat caps, new weapons, and class-specific skills.

See [classes-expanded.md](classes-expanded.md) for all class definitions and stat bonuses.

---

## Three-Tier Progression

```
Base Class (Lv1-15+)
  → Promoted Class (Lv15+ with promotion item)
    → Master Class (Lv30+ with Master Seal)
```

| Tier | Level Range | Stat Caps | Available From |
|------|-------------|-----------|---------------|
| **Base** | 1-20 (soft cap 15 for promotion) | HP 60, stats 20, LCK 30, CHA/WIL 15 | Ch1 |
| **Promoted** | 15+ (continues from base level) | HP 80, stats 30, LCK 40, CHA/WIL 20 | Arc 2 (Ch6+) |
| **Master** | 30+ (continues from promoted level) | HP 99, stats 35 (key stat 40), LCK 45, CHA/WIL 25 | Arc 4 (Ch18+) |

### Level Continuity

Promotion does **NOT** reset the unit's level. A Level 15 base class unit becomes a Level 15 promoted class unit. This means:
- No lost EXP from "waiting too long" to promote
- Promotes at 15 are optimal (most levels to grow in promoted caps)
- Promotes at 20 are acceptable (5 levels of capped stats, but stronger at promote time)

## Promotion Choices

Most base classes have **2 promotion options**. The player chooses at promotion time. The choice is permanent — cannot be undone.

### Choice Guidelines

| Base Class | Option A (balanced) | Option B (specialized) |
|-----------|-------------------|---------------------|
| Lord | Great Lord (+Lance, balanced) | Conqueror (+Axe, tank/CHA) |
| Cavalier | Paladin (balanced, +RES) | Great Knight (+Axe, full triangle, slower) |
| Mage | Sage (+Staff, attack+heal) | Mage Knight (+Sword, mounted) |
| Fighter | Warrior (+Bow, ranged option) | Berserker (pure crit, axe only) |
| Cleric | Bishop (+Light, attack+heal) | Valkyrie (+Light, mounted) |
| Soldier | General (+Sword, max DEF) | Halberdier (lance only, fast) |
| Archer | Sniper (pure accuracy/crit) | Nomad Trooper (+Sword, mounted) |
| Thief | Assassin (Lethality, knife only) | Rogue (+Sword, steal weapons) |
| Pegasus Knight | Falcon Knight (+Sword, fastest) | Dark Flier (+Dark, flying mage) |
| Wyvern Rider | Wyvern Lord (axe+lance, tank flier) | Malig Knight (+Dark, axe+dark) |
| Troubadour | Valkyrie (+Light, standard healer) | Maid (+Knife, debuff healer) |
| Mercenary | Hero (+Axe, all-rounder) | Swordmaster (sword only, crit king) |
| Shaman | Druid (+Staff, dark+heal) | Summoner (+Summon, phantom allies) |
| Monk | War Monk (+Staff+Axe, hybrid) | Saint (+Staff, pure caster) |
| Armor Knight | General (+Sword, immovable) | Great Knight (+Axe, mounted armor) |
| Dancer | — | **Cannot promote** |

### Exceptions

- **Dancer**: Cannot promote. Stays Dancer. This is intentional — Dance is so powerful it doesn't need promotion.
- **System Construct (Echo)**: Cannot promote normally. Stat caps are already 30 (promoted equivalent).
- **Akira**: Dies in Ch8 before promotion items are widely available (first at Ch7). If the player prioritizes promoting Akira early, they lose the item when he dies. Cruel but intentional.

## Stat Bonuses on Promotion

### Standard Bonuses (Tier 1)

Every promotion grants:
- **+2 HP** (baseline)
- **+1 MOV** (foot units only; mounted/flying already have high MOV)
- **2-4 points** distributed across key stats (varies by class — see classes-expanded.md)

### Master Bonuses (Tier 2)

Every master promotion grants:
- **+3 HP**
- **+1 MOV**
- **Unique class skill** (one per master class, automatically equipped, no slot cost)
- **1-2 points** to key stats

## Promotion × Story Pacing

### Expected Promotion Timeline

| Arc | Expected Player Levels | Promotion Status |
|-----|----------------------|-----------------|
| Arc 1 (Ch1-5) | 1-8 | Too early. No items available. |
| Arc 2 (Ch6-10) | 8-15 | **First promotions** (Ch7-10). Core units hit Lv15. |
| Arc 3 (Ch11-15) | 15-20 | Most units promoted. Late joiners catching up. |
| Arc 4 (Ch16-20) | 20-28 | All promoted. **First master promotions** (Ch18). |
| Arc 5 (Ch21-25) | 28-35 | 2-3 master classes active. Endgame power. |

### Akira's Promotion Dilemma

Akira can theoretically be promoted at Ch7 (Hero Crest available, Akira could be Lv15 with heavy EXP investment). But:
- He dies in Ch8 — the player loses the promotion item AND the promoted unit
- This creates a gut-punch moment: the player invested resources into making Akira stronger, and it was all for nothing
- OR the player learns (on repeat playthroughs) not to promote Akira, saving the item for Raiga/Goro
