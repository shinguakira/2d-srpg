# Zael — Wyvern Rider (The Ticking Bomb)

- **Class**: Wyvern Rider (axe/lance)
- **Awareness Tier**: 4 (Unaware — but CRP 40 means he FEELS it)

## Personality

Aggressive, impatient, scared underneath it all. The corruption makes him angry and irrational — he knows the anger isn't fully his but can't separate his emotions from the static. Fights it with raw willpower. Loud to drown out the buzzing. Charges in because standing still makes the noise worse.

## Meta-Angle

Unaware of cycles or the System, but CRP at 40 means he FEELS something wrong in a way no other Tier 4 character does. He can't articulate what's happening — just that there's a "buzzing in my skull" that gets louder near corruption. His body is a battlefield between his will and the System's degradation.

- "There's a buzzing in my skull. It gets louder when I stop moving."
- Glitched terrain makes his vision blur and his temper spike — he avoids it instinctively
- When CRP rises, his dialogue becomes shorter, angrier, less coherent
- If CRP hits 100, he turns enemy — the buzzing wins and he's gone

## What He Learns / What He Doesn't Know

- Learns that the "buzzing" is corruption — the System's failing code rewriting his data
- Discovers that Elara's light magic and Purify staves are the only things that push it back
- Grows to understand that his aggression is partly a symptom, and fighting smarter means fighting the corruption too
- Doesn't know that he's the party's canary in the coal mine — his CRP sensitivity warns others of danger zones

## Relationships

- **Yuel**: Fellow flier, opposite temperament. She's cautious and anxious; he's reckless and angry. They clash on tactics — she wants to scout, he wants to dive-bomb. But in the air, they cover each other instinctively. She's the only one who can keep up with him.
- **Elara**: Needs her purification desperately. She's the difference between Zael staying in the party and becoming an enemy. Their relationship starts transactional ("fix me") and grows into genuine gratitude and trust.
- **Bram**: Both aggressive frontliners. Bram channels chaos into joy; Zael channels it into fury. Bram is what Zael could be without the corruption — and Zael knows it.
- **Ren**: Zael resents Ren's calm strategizing. "Easy to plan when the buzzing isn't eating YOUR brain." But Ren's tactical placement keeps Zael away from corrupted terrain, and Zael slowly recognizes that as care.

## Arc

1. **Arc 3 (Ch13-15)**: Joins with CRP at 40 — already partially corrupted. Immediately one of the strongest physical units in the party, but a management challenge. Every corrupted tile is a risk.
2. **Arc 3-4 (Ch15-20)**: The party learns to manage his CRP. Elara's light magic, Purify staves, avoiding glitched terrain. Zael learns that accepting help isn't weakness.
3. **Arc 4 (Ch16-20)**: If managed well, CRP stabilizes. Zael's anger becomes focused instead of scattered. Promotes to Wyvern Lord or Malig Knight.
4. **Arc 5 (Ch21-25)**: Flies into the final battle with the buzzing still there — it never fully stops. But now he fights WITH it instead of against it. "The noise is part of me. I decide what it means."

## Key Lines

- *"There's a buzzing in my skull. Feels like the world is screaming and I'm the only one who hears it."*
- *"Don't tell me to calm down. You don't hear what I hear."*
- *"Elara. Do the light thing. Please."* (the "please" costs him everything)
- *"I can feel it trying to take over. It won't. I won't let it. Not today."*
