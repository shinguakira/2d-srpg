## Gold

### Sources

| Source | Gold Amount | Notes |
|--------|-----------|-------|

## Forging

Upgrade a weapon's stats with gold and materials. Available from **Arc 3** onward.

### Forging Tiers

| Tier | Cost | Material | Stat Boost |
|------|------|----------|-----------|
| **+1** | 1,000 gold + weapon | None | +1 Might, +5 Hit, +5 Crit (choose one) |
| **+2** | 2,500 gold + weapon | 1× Adamant | +2 Might, +10 Hit, +10 Crit (choose one) |
| **+3** | 5,000 gold + weapon | 1× Mithril | +3 Might, +15 Hit, +15 Crit (choose one) |

### Forging Rules

- Each weapon can only be forged **once** (choose tier at forging time, cannot upgrade incrementally)
- Forged weapons show a "+N" suffix (e.g., "Iron Sword+2")
- Prf weapons (Rapier, Garrison Lance, etc.) **cannot** be forged
- Brave weapons **cannot** be forged (already too strong)
- Can only forge ONE stat per weapon (Might OR Hit OR Crit, not all three)

### Forging Materials

| Material | Source | Arc |
|----------|--------|-----|
| **Adamant** | Treasure chests, enemy drops (rare) | Arc 3+ (2-3 per arc) |
| **Mithril** | Treasure chests only, 1-2 per arc | Arc 4+ (very rare) |

### Forging Strategy

- **Might forging**: Best for low-might weapons that need damage (Iron Sword+3 = 8 might, almost Silver tier at Iron cost)
- **Hit forging**: Best for inaccurate weapons (Iron Axe+3 = 90 hit, solving axes' accuracy problem)
- **Crit forging**: Best for already-good weapons that want kill potential (Killing Edge+3 = 45 crit — terrifying)

## Supply Convoy

A shared item storage accessible during preparation phase and at certain map tiles (armory/shop tiles).

### Rules

- Unlimited storage capacity
- All items not in unit inventories are automatically stored here
- Units can freely deposit/withdraw during preparation
- Mid-chapter access only at special tiles (if map has an armory)
- Weapons, items, forging materials, and promotion items all stored here

## Treasure Chests

Locked containers on maps. Require **Chest Key** (consumed on use) or **Thief's Lockpick** (reusable, Thief/Rogue only).

### Chest Contents by Arc

| Arc | Typical Contents |
|-----|-----------------|
| Arc 1 | Vulnerary, Iron weapons, 200-500 gold |
| Arc 2 | Steel weapons, promotion items, 500-800 gold |
| Arc 3 | Killer weapons, stat boosters, Adamant, 800-1200 gold |
| Arc 4 | Silver/Brave weapons, Master Crown, Mithril, 1000-1500 gold |
| Arc 5 | Legendary weapons, S-rank tomes, 1000-2000 gold |

### Enemy Thieves

Thief enemies will move toward and steal from treasure chests. If they steal the contents, the player must kill the thief to recover the item. Creates urgency — reach the chest first or chase the thief.

## Arena (Optional)

Available in some preparation phases (not every chapter). A training venue.

### Rules

- Bet gold (100-500) on a fight
- Unit fights a random enemy at similar level
- **Win**: Double your bet + unit gains EXP
- **Lose**: Lose your bet + unit takes damage (HP reduced to 1, NOT killed)
- Max 3 arena fights per unit per chapter (prevents grinding)
- Enemy difficulty scales with unit level
- Uses seeded RNG — same seed = same arena fights

### Design Intent

Arena is a safety valve for underleveled units. It's not efficient gold-farming (break-even at 50% win rate) but it IS efficient EXP for units that fell behind. The 3-fight limit and HP risk prevent abuse.

## Economic Balance Targets

### Player Spending Power by Arc

| Arc | Affordable Tier | Not Affordable |
|-----|----------------|---------------|
| Arc 1 | Iron weapons freely, 1-2 Steel | Silver, Killer, promotion items |
| Arc 2 | Steel freely, 1-2 promotions | Silver, Brave, forging |
| Arc 3 | Killer weapons, 2-3 promotions, basic forging | Brave, Master Crown, +3 forging |
| Arc 4 | Silver freely, Brave selectively, +2 forging | Full Brave loadout, multiple stat boosters |
| Arc 5 | Almost everything | Full +3 forged loadout (material-limited, not gold-limited) |

### Design Philosophy

- **Early game**: Gold is scarce. Iron weapons are free-ish but Steel is a meaningful purchase.
- **Mid game**: Gold flows but there are more things to buy (promotions, forging, better staves).
- **Late game**: Gold is plentiful but materials are the bottleneck. Mithril limits +3 forging more than gold does.
- **Overall**: The player should always have something they WANT to buy but can't afford yet. Never bankrupt, never swimming in gold.
