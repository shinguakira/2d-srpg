# Chapter 18 Map: "Legacy Weapons"

## Overview
- Grid size: 20×22
- Tileset: Wall, plain (stone floor), fort, door, chest, mountain, circuit, void, throne (fortified)
- Terrain focus: Mountain fortress with data-cracked walls. Treasure rooms flanking a central corridor. Ghael's defensive line bisects the map.

## Layout
A fortress carved into a mountainside. The south (rows 18-21) is the approach — mountain and road tiles leading to the main gate at (17, 10-11). The outer fortress (rows 12-17) has stone floor corridors with wall segments creating rooms. Two treasure rooms flank the corridor: west room (rows 14-16, cols 2-5) and east room (rows 14-16, cols 16-19), each behind a locked door. Ghael's defensive line runs across row 8 — a 2-tile-wide corridor with fort tiles at (8, 9) and (8, 12). He stands on the fortified tile at (8, 10) and does not move. Behind Ghael (rows 4-7) is the inner vault with two legacy weapon tiles at (5, 4) and (5, 17). The exit corridor runs north to (1, 10), blocked by a door at (3, 10). If Ghael is recruited, the Relic Warden spawns at (2, 10) to block the exit. Void patches at (6, 0), (6, 21) hint at data decay. Two forts at (12, 5) and (12, 16) anchor the outer fortress.

## Tactical Notes
- **Ghael's DEF wall**: 28 DEF + 4 terrain = 32 effective DEF. Physical attacks bounce. Magic is mandatory — Kanna (anima), Kira (dark), and Elara (light) are the primary damage sources. His RES is only 8.
- **Recruitment vs drops**: Recruiting Ghael (≤5 HP + Shigeru Talk) gives a powerful tank but spawns the Relic Warden as a secondary boss. Killing Ghael drops Master Crown + Fortress Lance. Either path awards a Master Crown — the difference is Ghael himself and the legendary weapon.
- **Treasure rooms**: Both side rooms are locked. Hachi can lockpick; Corridor Patrols drop keys. The Brave Axe (west) and Physic staff (east) are worth the detour. Send Hachi to one, use a key for the other.
- **Legacy weapons**: The Cycle Blade and Memory Tome are behind Ghael's line. Yuki can fly over to reach them early, but the Construct Sentries and Vault Mage will punish unsupported units.
- **Relic Warden**: Only appears if Ghael is recruited. High DEF (22), abysmal RES (6). Same magic-focused approach that worked on Ghael works here. The Warden blocks the exit — it must be defeated to complete the chapter.
