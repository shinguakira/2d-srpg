# Chapter 5: "Above the Clouds"

## Summary

Arc 1 climax. The party assaults a highland fortress held by General Aldric and the Empire's vanguard. Mid-battle, the world glitches for the first time — terrain shifts, tiles rewrite themselves, and a Data Void opens on the map. Yuel, a pegasus knight who witnessed sky anomalies, arrives with a warning nobody takes seriously enough. The comedy fades. The rules have changed.

## Prologue Dialogue

**Scene**: Dawn. The party camps on a ridge overlooking Skyhold Fortress. Clouds hang unnaturally low, clinging to the battlements. Yuel descends on her pegasus, lance drawn.

- **Yuel**: "DON'T go up there. Please. Something is wrong with the sky."
- **Ren**: "Wrong how?"
- **Yuel**: "The clouds were loading in SQUARES. Like tiles. I watched a patch of sky just... not render. For three seconds. Then it came back wrong."
- **Kael**: "Clouds don't load. They're clouds."
- **Yuel**: "I know what I saw."
- **Ren**: *(quietly)* "...I believe you."
- **Senna**: "The fortress is well-defended. General Aldric has knights on every approach. If we're doing this, we need the aerial route. Which means we need her."

**Yuel joins the party.**

## Mid-Battle Events

### Turn 3 — The Terrain Shifts

The party is halfway through the fortress approach when the ground changes. Forest tiles flicker and become plains. A wall tile vanishes, opening a new path. Then reappears. Then becomes water.

- **Bram**: "Did that TREE just turn into a RIVER?"
- **Senna**: "That's— no. That's impossible. Terrain values are fixed. I mapped this entire grid."
- **Ren**: "Senna. Your map."
- **Senna**: *(looking at her notes)* "It's... wrong now. My movement cost calculations are all wrong. The terrain changed AFTER I analyzed it."

**Mechanical effect**: 6 tiles on the map change terrain type. Movement costs and defense bonuses shift. Senna's pre-battle terrain data (shown in the prologue) no longer matches the grid.

### Turn 5 — The Data Void

A cluster of tiles in the northeast corner of the map go black. No terrain. No sprite. Just empty space. Units cannot enter. Enemies near it panic and scatter.

- **Yuel**: "THAT. That's what I saw in the sky. The nothing."
- **Coda**: "That's not 'nothing.' That's... unloaded. Like a texture that got deleted."
- **Lira**: "Can we fix it?"
- **Senna**: *(shaken)* "I don't— I can't analyze what isn't there. My formulas need INPUT. That void has no data."
- **Ren**: "Everyone stay away from it. Push toward Aldric."

**Mechanical effect**: A 3x2 block of impassable void tiles appears on the map. Enemies in the Guard AI zone near the void break formation and scatter to Aggressive.

### Turn 7 — Senna's Forecast Breaks

Senna attempts to calculate a combat forecast for an enemy soldier, and the numbers come out wrong. She runs the formula again. Same wrong answer.

- **Senna**: "Hit rate should be 87%. Damage should be 12. I've run the formula six times."
- **Ren**: "And?"
- **Senna**: "The system says 61% hit. 9 damage. The SEED changed. Mid-battle. That doesn't happen. The seed is set at the start and it NEVER changes."
- **Bram**: "Can we worry about math AFTER the guys with lances stop charging us?"
- **Senna**: "You don't understand. If the seed can change, NOTHING I've calculated this entire campaign is reliable."
- **Yuel**: "Is that why the sky broke? The same thing that's changing her numbers?"
- **Ren**: "Maybe. Push forward. We deal with Aldric first."

**Mechanical effect**: For one combat round, displayed hit rates flicker between two values before settling on the correct one. Visual-only — actual mechanics remain consistent.

## Boss Fight — General Aldric

General Aldric. General (lance/sword), Boss AI. Level 12, 50 HP. Very high DEF. Positioned on the throne tile with a Knight escort in adjacent tiles. Requires weapon advantage and careful positioning to break through the escort line.

- **Pre-combat**:
  - **Aldric**: "You've fought bandits and pirates. Now face a real army."
  - **Ren**: "You don't know what's happening to your fortress, do you?"
  - **Aldric**: "I know my walls hold. I know my men are loyal. I know you die here."
  - **Ren**: "Your walls just REWROTE themselves. Half your courtyard is a void. How are you not—"
  - **Aldric**: "I don't answer to anomalies. I answer to the Empire."

- **Post-combat**:
  - **Aldric**: *(defeated)* "A real army... and you still broke through."
  - **Ren**: "It wasn't us. Your fortress was falling apart before we got here."
  - **Aldric**: "...I noticed. I chose not to see it. Easier that way."

## Epilogue Dialogue

**Scene**: The fortress courtyard, after battle. The Data Void has closed, but the tiles it occupied are still wrong — plains where stone should be. The party stares at the gap in the world.

- **Senna**: "I need to say something. I've been tracking the combat seed since Chapter 1. Every random number, every hit roll, every level-up — I had the pattern mapped. All of it."
- **Ren**: "Had?"
- **Senna**: "It changed. Mid-battle. Something reached into the system and REWROTE the seed. My entire model is compromised."
- **Yuel**: "I told you. The sky was wrong."
- **Coda**: "And the ground. And the walls. And Senna's math."
- **Lira**: "So what does this mean? For us?"
- **Ren**: "It means the script isn't safe anymore. Whatever's running this world... it's editing in real time."
- **Kael**: "Then we adapt. We've fought bandits, pirates, and a general. We can handle a few broken tiles."
- **Ren**: *(not reassured)* "...Yeah. A few broken tiles."

## Story Beats Delivered

- Arc 1 climax: first Imperial General defeated, opening the path to Arc 2
- First major glitch event: terrain shifts mid-battle, Data Void appears on the map
- Yuel recruited: pegasus knight who witnessed sky anomalies, adds flying tactical option
- Senna's RNG seed analysis breaks: the system changed the seed mid-battle, destroying her predictive model
- Comedy-to-concern tonal shift: Bram's jokes land flat against genuine unease
- General Aldric as Tier 4 boss: refuses to acknowledge the anomalies even as his fortress rewrites itself
- The rules can change: established as the Arc 1 turning point — the script is no longer safe
- Kael's confidence foreshadows his Arc 2 fate: "We can handle it" while Ren knows otherwise
