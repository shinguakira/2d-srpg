# Rook — Mercenary (The Survivor)

- **Class**: Mercenary (sword)
- **Awareness Tier**: 2 (Partial — knows cycles, not the System)

## Personality

Cynical, world-weary, pragmatic. Speaks in flat declaratives. No wasted energy on hope or despair — just assessment. Has the exhaustion of someone who's been awake for 200 lifetimes and stopped expecting anything from any of them. Dry humor that's more dry than humor.

## Meta-Angle

200 cycles of awareness — less than Ren's 347 but enough to grind the idealism out of anyone. Knows the world resets. Knows people don't remember. Doesn't know WHY or that a System orchestrates it. Thinks loops are "just how reality works," like weather or gravity. Never tried to save anyone because why would you fix something that resets?

- "It loops. Always has. You fight, you die, it loops. What's to understand?"
- Sells his sword to whichever side seems most "real" this cycle — meaning most likely to do something new
- Joined Ren's party because Ren is the first person in 200 cycles to do something he hasn't seen before
- Treats his own death as an inconvenience, not a tragedy

## What He Learns / What He Doesn't Know

- Learns from Ren that there's a System behind the loops — his "just reality" assumption was wrong
- Discovers that his 200 cycles of passive survival made him complicit in the System's stagnation
- Learns to care about outcomes again — the hardest thing he's done in any cycle
- Doesn't know that his awareness was also a "bug" the System failed to patch, just like Ren's

## Relationships

- **Ren**: Both cycle-aware, opposite responses. Ren tried to optimize and save people; Rook just survived. They're mirrors — Ren shows Rook what caring costs, Rook shows Ren what giving up looks like. Neither likes what they see.
- **Voss**: Both former enemies who switched sides. Voss chose to change after 300 cycles of standing still; Rook chose to survive after 200 cycles of watching. They bond over the weight of wasted time.
- **Kael**: Rook is unmoved by Kael's death — he's seen hundreds of deaths. But later admits that Kael's willingness to be brave WITHOUT loop knowledge was something Rook never had.
- **Senna**: Rook's practical knowledge of cycle patterns feeds Senna's research. He gives data; she gives meaning.

## Arc

1. **Arc 2 (Ch6-10)**: Joins as a hired sword. "You're doing something new. That's worth my blade." Cold, transactional. Kael's death doesn't move him — visibly.
2. **Arc 3 (Ch11-15)**: Learns about the System from Senna and Ren. Realizes his "just reality" framing was a cope. The loops weren't natural — someone built them. His apathy was a choice, not wisdom.
3. **Arc 4 (Ch16-20)**: Begins actively fighting for the outcome. For the first time in 200 cycles, Rook wants something to matter. It's terrifying.
4. **Arc 5 (Ch21-25)**: Fights in the final battle not as a mercenary but as someone who chose a side. His last line isn't cynical — and that's how you know it's real.

## Key Lines

- *"I've seen this world end two hundred times. You get used to it. That's the worst part."*
- *"Ren tries to save everyone. I tried to save no one. Guess which one of us sleeps at night."*
- *"You're paying me in novelty. It's the only currency I can't earn by waiting."*
- *"...Fine. I'll care. But if this resets, I'm blaming you."*
