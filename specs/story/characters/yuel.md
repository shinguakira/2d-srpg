# Yuel — Pegasus Knight (The Sky Watcher)

- **Class**: Pegasus Knight (lance)
- **Awareness Tier**: 4 → 3 (grows — Unaware to Genre-Displaced)

## Personality

Anxious, hyper-vigilant, constantly scanning the horizon. Talks fast when nervous — which is always. Apologizes too much. Brave despite the fear, not because of its absence. She flies into danger while narrating every reason she shouldn't.

## Meta-Angle

Saw her homeland's sky glitch — clouds loading in visible square tiles. Can't unsee it. Starts at Tier 4 (unaware) but the memory nags at her constantly. She doesn't have a framework for what she saw, just a growing certainty that the world is wrong in a way she can't articulate.

- "The sky was... tiling. Like floor tiles but UP THERE."
- Keeps looking up mid-conversation, checking if the sky is still "normal"
- By Arc 3, starts connecting the sky glitch to corruption and asks the right questions
- Her awareness growth is organic — she pieces it together through observation, not revelation

## What She Learns / What She Doesn't Know

- Learns that the sky glitch was an early sign of System degradation — her homeland was the first region to destabilize
- Grows from "something is wrong" to "the world has rules that are breaking" by Arc 3
- Never reaches full Tier 2 understanding — she grasps the shape of the truth but not the details
- Doesn't know that her ability to see the glitch was itself unusual — most Tier 4 people can't perceive them at all

## Relationships

- **Kael**: Looks up to him — literally and figuratively. He's brave without hesitation; she's brave despite constant hesitation. His death in Ch8 devastates her. She takes over his role as the party's mobile frontliner and carries the weight of it.
- **Faye**: Fellow anxious soul. They bond over worry. Faye worries about people getting hurt; Yuel worries about the sky falling. Together they're a mess, but a supportive mess.
- **Nira**: Nira becomes her anchor. When Yuel spirals, Nira's quiet steadiness pulls her back.
- **Zael**: Fellow flier, opposite temperament. She's cautious; he's reckless. They argue about flight tactics but cover each other in the air.

## Arc

1. **Arc 1 (Ch5)**: Joins from a corrupted region. Grateful to be with people who don't think she's crazy for seeing the sky break. Nervous but determined.
2. **Arc 2 (Ch6-10)**: Kael's death forces her to step up. She replaces his mobility role — flying rescue, flanking, repositioning. The responsibility terrifies her but she doesn't flinch.
3. **Arc 3 (Ch11-15)**: Her awareness grows. The sky glitch, the corruption, the System — she starts connecting dots. "It's not just the sky. It's everything. The whole world is... loading."
4. **Arc 4-5 (Ch16-25)**: Flies into the final battles with open eyes. Still scared. Still checking the sky. But now she understands what she's seeing, and she fights anyway.

## Key Lines

- *"The clouds were squares. SQUARES. That's not — that's not how clouds work, right?"*
- *"I'm terrified. I'm always terrified. But I can still fly."*
- *"Kael would have charged in already. So I'll charge in for him. ...Sorry in advance."*
- *"The sky's breaking again. But I'm not looking away this time."*
