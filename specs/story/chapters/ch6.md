# Chapter 6: "New Alliances"

## Summary

The party pushes into coastal territory and is ambushed by Captain Sera's aerial patrol. A cynical mercenary named Rook and a sheltered troubadour named Faye join under very different circumstances — Rook for coin, Faye because people are bleeding and she can't walk past that. The objective splits: defeat Captain Sera to break the aerial blockade, then escape to the southern port before Imperial reinforcements cut off the retreat. The tone darkens as the CRP threat grows real.

## Prologue Dialogue

**Scene**: A coastal road. Cliffs to the east, open grassland to the west. A mercenary leans against a milestone marker, counting coins. Pegasus knight patrols circle overhead.

- **Rook**: "You the group that took Skyhold?"
- **Ren**: "Who's asking?"
- **Rook**: "Someone who just lost his employer to a void in the ground. General Aldric's gold dried up when his fortress started eating itself. I need new work."
- **Kael**: "We don't hire mercenaries."
- **Rook**: "Then I'll freelance. Same direction, same enemies. You don't pay me, I don't take orders. Fair?"
- **Ren**: "...Fine. Stay in lance range of Kael."

**Rook joins the party.**

- **Senna**: "Those pegasus patrols are Captain Sera's wing. She controls the airspace between here and the port. If we can't ground her, we can't escape south."
- **Yuel**: "I can match her in the air."
- **Ren**: "Not alone. She's got a full squadron. We fight through on the ground and force her down."

## Mid-Battle Events

### Turn 3 — Faye's Arrival

A troubadour rides onto the map from the eastern cliff road. An injured soldier is draped over the back of her horse.

- **Faye**: "Please — is anyone here a healer? This man was hit by a javelin and I can't stop the bleeding alone."
- **Lira**: "I'm a cleric! Bring him here."
- **Faye**: "Thank the stars. I found him on the road. I think he's one of yours?"
- **Voss**: "That's a border conscript. Not one of ours. Not one of anyone's."
- **Faye**: "People are hurting. I can help. That's enough, isn't it?"

**Faye joins the party** (arrives as a green NPC unit, auto-recruits when a player unit moves adjacent to her).

- **Rook**: "A healer who treats enemy soldiers. That's either very noble or very stupid."
- **Faye**: "Those aren't mutually exclusive."
- **Bram**: "I like her already."

### Turn 5 — Sera's Dive

Captain Sera breaks from patrol altitude and dives toward the party's position. Her movement range covers nearly the entire map.

- **Sera**: "You're earthbound. I decide when to fight."
- **Yuel**: "She's too fast in the air. We need to clip her range."
- **Nira**: "Bows. I can threaten her approach lanes. She can't dive into arrow range without taking the hit."
- **Ren**: "Nira, hold the center. Force her to commit to a lane. Yuel, intercept if she breaks through."

**Mechanical effect**: Captain Sera begins actively diving at player units. She retreats after attacking, using her full flying movement to reposition. Bow users within 2 tiles of her flight path force her to alter course, reducing her effective attack options.

### Turn 8 — The Reinforcement Warning

A scout reaches the party with news. Imperial infantry is approaching from the north.

- **Rook**: "That's a full column. Heavy armor. We have maybe four turns before they close the road."
- **Ren**: "Then we ground Sera and run. No time for a clean sweep."
- **Coda**: "The exit is south. Past the bridge. I scouted it — two guards, nothing we can't handle."
- **Senna**: "Unless the terrain decides to rewrite itself again."
- **Ren**: "Don't jinx it."

**Mechanical effect**: A turn counter appears. The party must defeat Captain Sera AND reach the southern escape tiles within 4 turns of the warning, or face overwhelming reinforcement spawns.

## Boss Fight — Captain Sera

Captain Sera. Pegasus Knight (lance), Aggressive AI. Level 12, 38 HP. Full flying mobility. Dives to attack, retreats to reposition. Must be grounded by bow damage or cornered against map edges. High SPD, low DEF — hits hard but can't take sustained punishment.

- **Pre-combat**:
  - **Sera**: "You took Aldric's fortress. Impressive. But Aldric was a wall. I'm the wind."
  - **Ren**: "Walls can be broken. Wind just needs a cage."
  - **Sera**: "Then build one. If you can."

- **Post-combat**:
  - **Sera**: *(grounded, lance broken)* "Earthbound tactics... against a flier. I underestimated you."
  - **Yuel**: "You fought well. For someone who fights alone."
  - **Sera**: "Alone is faster. Alone doesn't get people killed."
  - **Rook**: "Alone doesn't pay either. Trust me."

## Epilogue Dialogue

**Scene**: The southern port. Evening. The party made it past the bridge. Behind them, the Imperial column occupies the road they just fled. Close call.

- **Faye**: "Is it always like this? Running and fighting and running again?"
- **Lira**: "Sometimes there's a village visit in between. Those are nice."
- **Rook**: "So. The void. The terrain shifts. The sky loading in squares. Anyone want to explain what I signed up for?"
- **Ren**: "Something is wrong with the world. We don't know what yet."
- **Rook**: "Wonderful. And the pay?"
- **Ren**: "There is no pay."
- **Rook**: "...I'm going to regret this."
- **Senna**: "I've been running numbers on the corruption patterns from Ch5. Whatever changed the seed — it's spreading. The anomalies aren't random. They're accelerating."
- **Kael**: "Then we move faster. Whatever's ahead, we face it together."

## Story Beats Delivered

- Rook recruited: cynical mercenary, fights for gold, foil to Ren's idealism
- Faye recruited: warm mounted healer, Tier 4 unaware, joins because people need help
- Captain Sera as flying boss: teaches bow effectiveness and anti-air tactics in a boss context
- Defeat Boss + Escape objective: dual pressure of grounding Sera AND reaching exit before reinforcements
- Tonal darkening: new characters bring grounded perspectives that contrast with the party's growing unease
- CRP threat escalation: Senna confirms the Ch5 anomalies are spreading, not isolated
- Rook as audience surrogate: asks the questions a newcomer would about the world's instability
- Faye's compassion established: heals an enemy soldier without hesitation, contrasts with Rook's pragmatism
