# Chapter 21 Map: "The Long March"

## Overview
- Grid size: 20×22
- Tileset: Void, plain (unstable), forest (glitched), mountain (flickering), water (data stream), wall, circuit, road
- Terrain focus: Unstable terrain that shifts every 2 turns. Tiles change type in NW-to-SE wave patterns. Corridor collapse from behind forces forward momentum.

## Layout
An unfinished landscape inside the Blackflame's core. The south (rows 18-21) is the entry plateau — stable road tiles forming a 6-wide platform. The mid-section (rows 8-17) is the shifting zone: terrain tiles that randomly change type every 2 turns in a NW-to-SE wave. A rough path of circuit tiles runs diagonally from (17, 3) to (5, 18), providing the most reliable route. Scattered wall fragments create partial chokepoints at (14, 8-9) and (10, 13-14). Abyssal Rift spawn points dot the map at (12, 0), (12, 21), (8, 5), (8, 16) — portals that produce continuous reinforcements from Turn 4. The Revenant roams the northern zone (rows 4-8), appearing at (6, 11) on Turn 6. The exit is at (0, 19) — northeast corner. Starting from Turn 8, tiles collapse into void from the southwest corner, one row every 2 turns.

## Tactical Notes
- **Forward momentum is survival**: Continuous reinforcements from Abyssal Rift portals and corridor collapse from behind create a one-way pressure. Fighting every enemy is a trap. Kill what blocks the path, ignore what doesn't.
- **Terrain shift prediction**: Shifts sweep NW to SE every 2 turns. Watch northwest tiles — when they change, center tiles shift next turn. Echo senses shifts 1 turn early (passive ability).
- **Circuit path**: The diagonal circuit path from (17, 3) to (5, 18) is the most stable route. These tiles never shift. Use them as waypoints for safe movement.
- **Flier advantage**: Elin (and Zael if recruited) ignore terrain shifts and corridor collapse. They can scout ahead, engage the Revenant early, or rescue stranded ground units.
- **The Revenant**: Extreme speed (28) means most units cannot double it. Its erratic AI is a double-edged sword — it rarely focuses one unit but is hard to predict. High-DEF tanks (Ghael, Halvar) absorb its attacks while axe users exploit weapon advantage.
- **Escape timing**: The exit at (0, 19) should be reached by Turn 12-14. After Turn 10, collapse accelerates. Shigeru must be the one to reach the exit tile — plan his path carefully.
- **Sandstorm weather**: +1 movement cost on sand tiles (some tiles shift to sand), -10 hit for all units. Accuracy-dependent units (Bryn, Fenn) suffer most. Compensate with weapon triangle advantage for hit bonuses.
- **Mithril reward**: The Revenant drops Mithril on defeat — an endgame forging material. Worth engaging the boss rather than bypassing, but don't overcommit if the corridor collapse is closing in.
- **Echo's passive**: Echo senses terrain shifts 1 turn early. Keep Echo near the main group so the party can react to incoming shifts. Echo's warnings are displayed as event text at the start of each turn.
- **Data Fragment enemies**: The initial three corrupted soldiers at row 14 are weak but positioned to block all three movement lanes. Clear them on Turn 1-2 before the void spawns begin. Gareth and Corwin can one-round them.
- **No boss requirement**: The Revenant is not required for victory — only Shigeru reaching the exit matters. Engaging the Revenant is optional but rewarded (Mithril + EXP). If the collapse timer is tight, bypass the boss entirely with fliers carrying Shigeru.
