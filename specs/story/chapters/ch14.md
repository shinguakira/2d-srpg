# Chapter 14: The Sand Queen

*Not yet implemented.*

## Summary

West into the desert reaches, to the court of a queen the war has not touched and
who has no reason to care — until Kanna shows her which way the grey is moving.

## Intent

- **Objective**: defend the oasis
- **New terrain**: sand (movement cost 2, avoid penalty). Cavalry is punished, so the
  chapter forces the player to fight with units they have been benching.
- **The first ally who is not a stray.** Every recruit so far has been someone with
  nowhere else to go. Here Shigeru has to argue, as the heir of a kingdom that no
  longer exists, for a war the queen can still afford to sit out.
- **Boss**: a Kurogane expeditionary force that arrived first and made the same
  argument with better manners and more gold.
