# Chapter 12: "The Turning"

## Summary

The darkest chapter yet. A party member — whichever unit has the highest CRP — hits 100 corruption mid-battle and turns hostile. They cannot be saved. The chapter is a defense of a corrupted waystation against waves of corrupted enemies led by Abbot Seras, a Bishop who believes corruption is purification. The corruption loss happens as a mid-battle event: the turned ally attacks their former comrades, must be put down, and is gone forever. Seras is the main boss — a partially aware zealot who heals corrupted enemies and uses light magic that specifically damages corrupted party members. The party leaves with one fewer member and the understanding that CRP can kill.

## Mid-Battle Events

### Turn 3 — Seras Arrives

Abbot Seras steps onto the northern edge of the map, flanked by corrupted acolytes. He carries a light tome and a Physic staff. His robes are pristine white. His eyes glow faintly.

- **Seras**: "Children of the broken world. I come to CLEANSE you."
- **Ren**: "You're corrupted yourself."
- **Seras**: "Corrupted? No. ELEVATED. The pattern showed me what lies beneath the data — the truth of this world. It must be PURIFIED. Burned down to code and rebuilt."
- **Senna**: "He's Tier 2. He's partially aware of the System, but he thinks corruption IS the System's intent."
- **Seras**: "I have seen the truth behind the pattern. You MUST be cleansed."
- **Kira**: "I used to believe the same thing. He's too far gone."

**Mechanical note**: Seras heals corrupted enemies with his Physic staff from range. His light magic deals bonus damage to any party member with CRP > 0. Priority: neutralize Seras's healing or the corrupted enemies become a war of attrition.

## Boss Fight — Abbot Seras

Abbot Seras. Bishop (light/staff), Boss AI, Tier 2 (Partial awareness). Level 20, 50 HP. Positioned near the northern shrine. Heals corrupted allies with Physic. Light magic deals bonus damage to party members with any CRP. Surrounded by 2 corrupted acolytes.

- **Pre-combat**:
  - **Seras**: "You cling to the old data. The corrupted world. Can't you see? The System is REWRITING us into something better."
  - **Ren**: "It's not rewriting. It's ERASING. I just watched it erase someone I swore to protect."
  - **Seras**: "A small price for transcendence."
  - **Ren**: "There's no transcendence. There's just deletion."

**Mechanical note**: Seras's light magic punishes corrupted party members — Kira (CRP 60+) takes massive bonus damage and should not engage directly. Non-corrupted units deal normal damage. Seras uses Physic to heal corrupted minions, so the player should either rush him or eliminate the acolytes first to stop the healing chain. His position near the shrine gives +3 DEF/RES.

- **Post-combat**:
  - **Seras**: *(dying)* "You don't understand yet. You will. When the corruption reaches you — ALL of you — you'll see what I saw. The pattern is beautiful."
  - **Kira**: "I saw the pattern. It's not beautiful. It's just code."
  - **Seras**: "...Then why does it sing?"
  - *(He dissolves. The corrupted acolytes collapse.)*

## Story Beats Delivered

- First corruption permadeath — a party member turns hostile and must be killed by the player
- CRP established as a lethal mechanic, not just a debuff — hitting 100 means permanent loss
- Abbot Seras represents the "corruption as enlightenment" faction — partially aware zealot who misreads the System's intent
- Light magic's dual nature introduced: effective against corruption, but Seras weaponizes it against corrupted allies
- Kira's past as a corruption spreader given weight — she recognizes Seras's delusion because she shared it
- The emotional difference between "lost in battle" (Kael) and "had to put down" (corruption loss) explored
- Senna's CRP tracking establishes that the entire party is slowly accumulating corruption — ticking clock
- Urgency for the monastery and purification mechanics set up Ch13-14
- Tone: the darkest chapter. Gallows humor is gone. Even Orin drops the act.
