# Chapter 2: The Defector

Exact dialogue lives in `src/data/chapters/chapter2.ts`.

## Summary

The Kuze river garrison — the only bridge for thirty miles. Introduces a recruitable
enemy, chokepoint tactics, and the campaign's real plot: a **cracked shrine ward**
taken off the enemy commander.

## Setup

- **Map**: 18×10, bridge chokepoint plus a shallow-water flank, throne at (7,0)
- **Objective**: Seize the throne
- **Deploy**: 5 slots, `shigeru` forced
- **Boss**: Commander Ryuji
- **Recruitable**: `genzo` — placed as a stationary enemy, defects on turn 3
- **Par**: 10 turns

## Beats

**Prologue.** Kanna identifies Ryuji's banner from memory (she catalogued every
heraldic device in the royal archive at fourteen, "it was that or conversation").
Ryuji will not come out; the party has to go in.

**Turn 2 — the bridge.** Akira wants the bridge, Kanna wants the water flank. Both
are correct, which is the point of the map. Kanna also notes a wall sentry who has
not turned his head — Genzo, about to break.

**Turn 3 — Genzo defects.** Sergeant, second wall company, eleven years on a watch
post. Three weeks ago his own army burned a village he could see from his post, and
he did not leave his post, because that is what a good soldier does. He has thought
about it for three weeks and would rather be a bad soldier.

Goro: *"That is either the bravest thing I have heard or the stupidest."*
Genzo: *"It is both, lad. Most true things are."*

**Turn 4 — the first mark.** Kanna finds a two-pace-wide line of grey grass along
the north wall, arrow-straight, bearing north-north-east. No scorch, no rot, no
insects. Hina names it from a shrine song — *ashfall* — and Kanna refuses to accept
a song as evidence while accepting the straight line as evidence.

**Boss pre-combat.** Shigeru tells Ryuji his emperor broke the northern shrine.
Ryuji's answer is the campaign's recurring note: *"A soldier who reads past his
orders is a soldier looking for a reason to run."*

**Boss death.** Genzo's eulogy for him: not a cruel man, just one who never looked
up from his orders — *"that is most of the men we are going to have to kill."*

**Epilogue — the plot starts.** Ryuji was carrying a shrine ward, cracked clean
through. Wards are cut from a single stone precisely so they cannot crack. Genzo
supplies the missing half: Takeshi went to the northern shrine alone before the war
and came back three days later and gave the order to march. Nobody asked what he did
there. *"You do not ask Takeshi things."*

## Supports

- **Shigeru × Kanna** — she tells him to ask for the numbers *before* deciding
  rather than after, and admits she is bad at consoling people.
- **Akira × Hina** — she takes his watch post off him so he will sit down.
