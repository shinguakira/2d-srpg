# Chapter 25 Map: "The Last Save File"

## Overview
- Grid size: 24×28
- Tileset: Void, circuit, wall, plain (data floor), throne (System core), data_node, archive_tile
- Terrain focus: Three-phase map. Phase 1: maze of data walls surrounding the core. Phase 2: walls corrupt into void, map shrinks. Phase 3: all terrain flattens — open arena with the throne at center. The largest and final map.

## Layout
The System's heart. A 24×28 grid — the largest map in the game. **Phase 1 layout**: A maze of wall segments fills the map. The south (rows 22-27) is the deployment zone — open data floor. Wall corridors run north in three paths: west (cols 2-6), center (cols 10-14), east (cols 18-22). Corridors twist and branch at rows 16, 12, and 8. Dead ends hold data_node tiles at (16, 4), (12, 20), (8, 8). The System's throne sits at (2, 12) behind the deepest walls. Forts at (20, 6), (20, 18), (12, 12). **Phase 2 (triggered at 80 HP)**: Walls begin corrupting to void. 3 wall tiles become void per turn, starting from edges. Ghost boss phantoms spawn from corrupted tiles. The maze opens as walls fall but void tiles deal damage. **Phase 3 (triggered at 40 HP)**: All remaining walls vanish. The entire map becomes open data floor. Only the throne remains. Corruption storm weather: +1 CRP/turn to all units.

## Tactical Notes
- **Phase 1 — the maze**: Three corridors lead north. The center corridor is shortest but has the Construct Assassin. West and east are longer but have data_node buffs in dead ends. Yuel can fly over walls to scout or bypass. Recommended: split into two groups (west + center, east + center) to converge on the throne.
- **Data node buffs**: Each data_node awards +2 all stats to the capturing unit. With three nodes, that's three significantly boosted units — worth the detour if you have the action economy.
- **Phase 2 — corruption race**: When walls become void, the maze opens but the floor becomes lethal. Ghost bosses spawn one per turn as distractions. Focus the System — it has left the throne and become aggressive. Its +2 MOV makes it dangerous. Corruption storm (+1 CRP/turn) creates a soft timer.
- **Phase 3 — no combat**: At 40 HP, the System stops fighting. All enemies freeze. The map flattens to open ground. Ren walks to the throne and uses the Final Save Crystal. There is no mechanical challenge here — the challenge was reaching this point.
- **Army composition**: Bring your strongest 12. Every weapon type is needed (the System uses all types). Healers are essential for Phase 2 corruption damage. Orin's Dance is invaluable for burst damage against the System in Phases 1-2.
- **Final map, final resources**: This is the last chapter. Use every consumable, every legendary weapon, every remaining charge. Nothing carries over. Hold nothing back.
- **Ghost boss distractions**: Phase 2 ghost bosses cycle through Garrek, Thane, Morryn, Aldric, Drayen, and Ghast at 50% original stats. They follow their original AI patterns. Ignore them if possible — the System is the only target that matters.
- **Corruption storm**: +1 CRP per turn to all units during Phase 2. High-CRP units risk hitting thresholds that affect performance. Elara's light magic can cleanse 5 CRP per heal action — keep her active.
