# Chapter 6: The Harbour at Kechi

Exact dialogue lives in `src/data/chapters/chapter6.ts`.

## Summary

**Arc 2 opener.** A coastal harbour held by a Kurogane patrol with air cover. Two
recruits, escalating reinforcement waves, and the news that the blight has already
gone *past* the party in the night — the south road is cut, so turning north stops
being a choice.

## Setup

- **Map**: 16×18, harbour town, cliffs, two bridges, water channel
- **Objective**: Defeat Captain Tsubame
- **Deploy**: 7 slots, `shigeru` forced
- **Boss**: Captain Tsubame (pegasus knight, aggressive)
- **Joins**: `raiga` (prologue), `mio` (turn 4)
- **Reinforcements**: turn 8 heavy cavalry ×4; turn 12 a full column ×6
- **Par**: 16 turns

## Beats

**Prologue — the fifth mark.** Raiga's last contract was guarding a caravan out of
the highlands. There is no caravan and there are no highlands: grey ground where the
road was, nothing to walk back to. Kanna realises it has passed them in the night —
it is between them and the south road. Raiga signs on because everyone else on the
coast is running from the north and these are the only fools walking at it.

**Turn 4 — Mio.** A mounted healer treating a Kurogane soldier mid-battle. Akira
asks if she is one of theirs; Hina answers *"She is on the side of the hurt. I know
that side."*

**Turns 8 and 12 — the waves.** Mechanical pressure, not story: the chapter should
feel like a fight the party wins by finishing on time rather than by killing
everything.

**Boss.** Tsubame is beaten out of the air by Yuki and takes it professionally, then
delivers the arc's political thesis: she reported the hole in the sky as weather
twice, and the second report came back with her commission attached and a note
telling her to fly lower. *"Then you are braver than my whole wing, boy, and I hope
somebody writes it down."*

She is written to be recruitable later (Arc 4, `tsubameRecruited`).

**Epilogue — why they fight.** Mio asks why the Kurogane boy she treated is
fighting at all. Raiga gives the cynic's answer; Genzo corrects him, and it is the
most important line in the arc:

> *"He is fighting because Takeshi told him the war would be the last one. Every
> soldier in Kurogane believes that. It is why they march so well."*

Asked whether he still believes it, Genzo says he believes *Takeshi* believes it —
*"that is the part that frightens me."*

## Supports

- **Shigeru × Raiga** — Raiga has served eleven captains; the good ones counted
  their dead by name for about a year and then stopped. Shigeru orders him to say so
  when it starts happening.
- **Hina × Mio** — two healers with different theologies of the same job. *"You
  guard the soul, I will guard the body."*
