# Chapter 16: The Man Who Filed Nothing

*Arc 4 opener. Not yet implemented.*

## Summary

Into Kurogane-held ground around **Ilza**, where the occupying governor has kept
the grey out of his emperor's sight for a year by the simple method of not filing
the reports.

## Intent

- **Objective**: seize
- **The reveal**: the ground the Empire took is going grey behind its own front line,
  and its officials have all made the same choice Roderic, Olrik and Varro made —
  hold the post, do not look west. The pattern the player has been meeting since Ch3
  turns out to go all the way to the top.
- **Boss**: the governor, who is not mad at all. He has been buying his district time
  by lying to Takeshi, and he is the first enemy in the campaign who begs the party
  to keep going after he loses.
- **Weight**: this is the chapter where the party walks back into the burnt capital.
