# Faye — Troubadour (The Stubborn Healer)

- **Class**: Troubadour (staff)
- **Awareness Tier**: 4 (Unaware)

## Personality

Warm, earnest, stubborn. Speaks with conviction that borders on defiance. She chose to ride into battle as a healer — nobody drafted her, nobody asked her, she just showed up on a horse with a staff and refused to leave. Argues with kindness as her weapon of choice.

## Meta-Angle

Completely unaware of cycles, the System, or any meta-reality. Doesn't know. Doesn't care. "People are hurting. I can help. That's enough." Her unawareness isn't ignorance — it's moral clarity. She doesn't need cosmic context to justify doing the right thing.

- When told about cycles: "So people have been hurting for THAT long and nobody helped?"
- Treats the metaphysical crisis as a medical one — "The world is sick. Sick things can be healed."
- Her conviction is so strong it makes aware characters question their own nihilism
- Accidentally delivers profound truths by applying healer logic to existential problems

## What She Learns / What She Doesn't Know

- Learns enough about cycles to understand the stakes, but it doesn't change her behavior at all
- Discovers that her healing staff interacts with corruption in unexpected ways — care itself has mechanical weight
- Never fully grasps the System or meta-awareness, and it genuinely doesn't matter for her arc
- Learns that sometimes the bravest thing isn't charging in — it's staying close enough to heal the ones who do

## Relationships

- **Lira**: Fellow healer, different philosophy. Lira heals through emotional support and romance-game framing; Faye heals through stubborn refusal to let people die. They argue about methods but their supports are deeply warm.
- **Kael**: Respects his bravery. They overlap for only a few chapters (Ch6-8) but she sees in him the same "charge in and help" instinct she has. His death hardens her resolve.
- **Yuel**: Fellow anxious soul. Faye worries about injuries; Yuel worries about the sky. They comfort each other in a "we're both a mess but at least we're a mess together" way.
- **Bram**: Faye spends half her time healing Bram's reckless injuries and the other half lecturing him about self-preservation. He never listens. She never stops.

## Arc

1. **Arc 2 (Ch6-10)**: Joins with Rook but for opposite reasons. He's there for novelty; she's there because people are hurt. Immediately fills the mobile support role. Kael's death hits her hard.
2. **Arc 3 (Ch11-15)**: Corruption threatens the party. Faye approaches it like any illness — "What's the treatment?" Her staff work becomes critical for managing CRP.
3. **Arc 4 (Ch16-20)**: Promotes to Valkyrie, gaining light magic. Now she can fight AND heal. "I didn't come this far to stand in the back."
4. **Arc 5 (Ch21-25)**: Rides into the final battle as she rode into the first — by choice. Her closing line is the emotional thesis of her arc.

## Key Lines

- *"People are hurting. I can help. That's enough reason."*
- *"You can explain the loops later. Right now, hold still — you're bleeding."*
- *"I don't need to understand the world to know it's worth saving."*
- *"Kael charged forward so others wouldn't have to. I ride forward so the ones who charge can come back."*
