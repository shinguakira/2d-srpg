# Kira — Shaman (The Defector)

- **Class**: Shaman (dark tomes)
- **Awareness Tier**: 2 (Partial)

## Personality

Quiet guilt. Speaks softly, precisely, like someone choosing every word to minimize the damage it might cause. Was a dark mage spreading corruption before defecting. Carries the weight of what she did in every sentence. Not self-pitying — self-aware. The difference is she still acts despite the guilt.

## Meta-Angle

Understood the corruption data better than most — that's why she could spread it so effectively. Her partial awareness came FROM the corruption: by working with the System's dark code, she learned its patterns. Now she wants to undo what she built. Knows the System exists but not its purpose.

- "I could read the corruption because I was writing it. I knew the syntax."
- Recognizes glitched terrain as "familiar" — she used to CREATE those patterns
- Her dark magic is literally the same force as corruption, just directed by will instead of System automation
- The party's distrust of her is earned and she accepts it without complaint

## What She Learns / What She Doesn't Know

- Learns that the corruption she spread wasn't malice from the System — it was the System failing, and she was accelerating the failure
- Discovers her Nosferatu drain works because she's recycling corrupted energy into life force
- Grows to understand that guilt without action is just another form of stagnation
- Doesn't know that her defection was one of the System's first "unexpected events" — she was an early bug, like Ren

## Relationships

- **Elara**: Opposite magic, mutual respect. Light and dark, purification and drain. Elara doesn't judge Kira's past — she judges her present actions, and finds them worthy. Their combined magic is the party's strongest anti-corruption tool.
- **Senna**: Data vs guilt. Senna wants Kira's corruption knowledge for research; Kira finds it painful to discuss. Over time, Senna learns to ask gently and Kira learns that sharing her knowledge helps undo the damage.
- **Ren**: Both carry the weight of too many cycles. Kira spread corruption across dozens of loops before defecting. Ren tried to save people across hundreds. They understand each other's exhaustion.
- **Zael**: Kira feels responsible for Zael's corruption — she may have spread the very taint that's killing him. Helping manage his CRP is personal atonement.

## Arc

1. **Arc 3 (Ch11-15)**: Defects from the corruption forces. The party doesn't trust her. She doesn't expect them to. Proves herself through action — her Nosferatu sustains the frontline, her knowledge maps the corruption spread.
2. **Arc 3-4 (Ch15-20)**: Earns trust slowly. Her corruption expertise becomes critical for navigating the System's architecture. Elara and Kira develop the light/dark synergy that counters corruption.
3. **Arc 4 (Ch16-20)**: Confronts her former allies. Some she corrupted. Some she trained. Every fight is personal.
4. **Arc 5 (Ch21-25)**: Uses her understanding of corruption to help dismantle the System from within. "I built the poison. I can build the antidote."

## Key Lines

- *"I know what the corruption looks like from the inside. I put it there."*
- *"Don't forgive me. Let me earn something better than forgiveness."*
- *"Nosferatu doesn't heal. It recycles. Everything I take was something I broke first."*
- *"Elara purifies. I drain. Between us, the corruption has nowhere to hide."*
