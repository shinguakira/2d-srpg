# Chapter 7: What the Wall Held

Exact dialogue lives in `src/data/chapters/chapter7.ts`.

## Summary

**Kanna's chapter.** A survive-12-turns defence of the coastal fortress while
revenants come up out of the north wall. Introduces the Blackflame's units, and
breaks its central rule of behaviour: it stops acting like weather and starts acting
like something that notices being measured.

## Setup

- **Map**: 16×16, fortress interior, corridors, blighted north edge
- **Objective**: Survive 12 turns
- **Deploy**: 7 slots, `shigeru` forced
- **Optional boss**: Admiral Isonami (stationary) — drops a Hero Crest
- **Reinforcements**: revenant waves on turns 3, 5, 7, 9, out of the north wall

## Beats

**Prologue.** Kanna has been at the ward readings all night. Eleven times in a row
she has charted where the blight will surface, and eleven times it has surfaced
somewhere else. She asks for it to be said aloud, before it matters, that her eyes
may be worth very little today.

**Turn 1.** The ward-stone has gone cold — either the north is empty, or the thing
is no longer in the north.

**Turn 3 — revenants.** They climb out of the grey stone. Goro sees one wearing
Amagi colours. Hina names them and gives the instruction that defines them for the
rest of the game: *"The Blackflame does not kill men, it hollows them and stands
them back up. Do not look at their faces."*

**Turn 6 — the breakdown.** Kanna calls the east stair; it comes up the west. She
calls the west; it comes through the floor. *"It is not outrunning me — it is waiting
to hear what I say."*

Her crisis is identity, not competence: *"My whole use to this company is knowing. If
I cannot know, what am I standing here for?"*

**Raiga supplies the field fix**, not a comfort: stop calling it out loud, write it
down and hand it to the prince — *"if the cursed thing is listening, make it work for
what it hears."* Kanna calls it a horrible idea and asks for his chalk.

**Turns 9-12.** The waves thin. The fortress holds. The relief is deliberately flat.

**Epilogue — the resolution.** Kanna has six years of ward theory built on the one
thing everyone agrees on: that a seal does not think. It thinks.

Shigeru's answer is the chapter's point — *stop asking where it will go; ask what it
wants* — and Hina defends the move: *"That is not a measurement." "No. It is a
question. Those are allowed too."*

That night Kanna burns two years of charts and starts a new book with one line at the
top of it: **WHAT DOES IT WANT?**

## Supports

- **Shigeru × Kanna** — she held the ladder, badly, when he fell off it in the
  archive at seven. His father said she had the only interesting question in the
  room. She resolves to find another one.
- **Kanna × Hachi** — a thief's method for a house that lies to you: stop asking
  what is behind the door, and watch which door the owner never opens. *"Things give
  themselves away by what they protect."* Kanna resents how useful this is.
